<h2 align="center">论述型作文·图画作文高分速成</h2>

> **本讲核心**：速成 = **背框架 → 套主题词**。考场只做两件事——**审题立意**（定主题词/中心句、定描图要点、定论述框架与角度）与 **代入英文框架填充成文**；框架、模板句全部提前背好。全讲给三套东西：① 三段**布局表**（句数/字数）② 每段**可填空模板**（首段 2 版、中间段 3 类、尾段 2 类）③ **两套实战演练**（抽象主题"创新"＋负向现象"环保"）。
> **与相邻两页的分工**：本文是**考场速成版模板**（框架 + 填充）；`论述型作文-三段写作模板.md` 是通用三段骨架与范文；`写作句式.md` 按功能给全部可替换句式。

### （一）速成总流程与三段时间分配

**图画作文高分速成步骤**：

1. **审题立意**（2 分钟）
2. **编列提纲**（3 分钟）
3. **布局 + 成文**（30 分钟）

**考场优化步骤**（布局与写作框架已提前背熟）：

```
布局 & 写作框架已定（背模板）
→ 审题立意 → 编列提纲
→ 套用框架成文
```

**审题立意三步**（全讲收尾页的总结）：

1. **确定主题词或中心句**——先判是**反映现象**还是**陈述观点**
2. **确定首段描图要点**
3. **确定论述框架和论述角度** → 代入英文框架，**填充成文**

> **一句话**：考场时间不是用来"想句式"的，而是用来**审题 + 选框架 + 填主题词**。

### （二）三段高分布局（句数与字数）

| <nobr>段落</nobr> | <nobr>句数</nobr> | <nobr>字数</nobr> | <nobr>内容分工</nobr> |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| <nobr>起始段</nobr> | 3-4 | 40-50 | Part1 如图所示，概述图画<br>Part2 围绕主题，细节描写<br>Part3 借助中文，引向主题 |
| <nobr>中间段</nobr> | 5-8 | 80-100 | Part1 揭示主题，统领下文<br>Part2 围绕主题，展开论证 |
| <nobr>结尾段</nobr> | 2-3 | 30-40 | 重申主题 / 提出建议，展望未来 |

> **口径**：三段合计约 150~190 词，落在 Directions 的 160-200 词区间内；**中间段是唯一必须"写满"的段落**，首尾都可以用模板句顶住。

### （三）1.1 首段高分框架（两版模板）

#### 一、一幅图模板

1. **As is vividly depicted in the picture, 整体描述.**
2. **The most striking feature is that 细节描述.**
3. **There is no doubt that its symbolic / intended meaning subtly conveyed should be given a deep consideration.**

#### 二、两幅图模板

1. **As the picture here conspicuously depicts, 整体描述.**
2. **In the first drawing, 第一幅图画细节描述. By contrast, in the second picture, 第二幅图画细节描述.**
3. **We are informed that 文字说明.**

> **兼容说明**：本讲首段走**三句版**（总述 → 最显著特征 → 意义深思），与《图画作文首段》的**四句框架**（总体描述 → 细节 1 → 细节 2 → 文字说明）并不冲突——**单图细节少就用三句版，双图/细节多就用四句框架**；三句版的第 ③ 句相当于"搭桥"，四句框架的文字说明可改写为 `We are informed that ...`。
> **易错点**：`There is no doubt that ... should be given a deep consideration` 中 **be given 是被动**（寓意"被"思考）；`its symbolic meaning subtly conveyed` 中 **conveyed 是过去分词后置定语**（"被传递的"寓意）。

### （四）1.2 中间段高分框架（三类模板）

#### 一、先选框架：按主题类型三选一

```
中间段三类框架模板
├─ ① 原因分析   ← 负面现象（问题、不良风气）
├─ ② 意义分析   ← 正面现象（品质、好做法）
└─ ③ 利弊分析   ← 两面性话题（科技、AI 等"双刃剑"）
```

> **判据**：主题词是**问题**（污染、失礼、不诚信）→ 原因分析；是**品质/好做法**（创新、坚持、环保意识）→ 意义分析；是**中性事物**（AI、网络、手机）→ 利弊分析。

#### 二、模板 A：原因分析（①引出问题 → ②设问 → ③总述 → ④⑤⑥三条原因）

1. **We are supposed to place our attention on, instead of its (funny) appearance, the phenomenon reflected / revealed in the cartoon that some people pay little attention to sth.**
2. **Why does this phenomenon appear?**
3. **Several possible reasons for it can be figured out.**
4. **First and foremost, the phenomenon may be closely related to the fact that + 句子.**
5. **Furthermore, 原因二 can also contribute to some extent to 主题.**
6. **Last but not least, it is speculated that perhaps the current situation can be ascribed to 原因三.**

> **技巧**：④⑤⑥ 就是**内因 → 外因**两条腿（老师原话"一般我们都从内因和外因两大角度"）——内因（个人意识、自私、道德）走 ④，外因（媒体宣传、监管、制度）走 ⑤⑥。

#### 三、模板 B：意义分析（②③④三层）

1. **First and foremost, it is universally held that sth contributes a lot to 意义一. For example, with sth, we are more likely to do sth.**
2. **In the second place, we all share an idea that sth also plays a vital role in 意义二, which can be easily demonstrated by the fact that + 句子.**
3. **Last but not least, according to some research findings, by doing sth, there is a high possibility that we could do sth.**

> **技巧**：三条意义按**由近及远**排——个人（individual growth）→ 社会（social progress）→ 科技/人类（science and technology）；每条都自带"证明"手段：`For example` / `demonstrated by the fact that` / `according to some research findings`。

#### 四、模板 C：利弊分析（①引言 → ②弊 → ③个人角度 → ④社会角度）

1. **The picture is fairly thought-provoking, which intends to reveal a much-discussed question about the advantages and disadvantages of sth. As far as I am concerned, we cannot tell whether sth is good or bad unless we properly take advantage of it.**
2. **Improper use or even misuse of sth will of course result in some negative impacts on us.**
3. **Judging from individual perspective, it may lead to sth. For example, when we ignore the possible undesirable influence of sth, we may be faced with ... .**
4. **Judging from the social angle, the development of sth without supervision from departments concerned or reasonable restrictions by rules is bound to harm social interests and hinder social progress.**

> **技巧**：利弊段的立场句是 ① 的 `we cannot tell whether sth is good or bad **unless** we properly take advantage of it`——**不站队、只谈"善用"**，比"利弊各打五十大板"更高级。

#### 五、填充角度（把 sth 换成主题词后，从这里取内容）

**具体意义分析角度**：

| <nobr>角度</nobr> | 可填内容 |
|:---|:---|
| <nobr>个人发展</nobr> | 目标 / 梦想 / 成功 / 自我实现 |
| <nobr>三子角度</nobr> | ① 获得 / 迎接 / 抓住机遇　② 面对 / 应对困难、挑战、压力　③ 又快又好——效率 / 效果 |

**具体原因分析常见维度**（讲义带英文对照）：

| <nobr>层面</nobr> | 中文落点 | 英文表达 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| <nobr>个人</nobr> | 自私自利 | selfish |
| <nobr>个人</nobr> | 道德缺失 | moral decline / absence of social morality |
| <nobr>个人</nobr> | 意识不足 | insufficient awareness |
| <nobr>社会</nobr> | 制度不完善 | systems with flaws |
| <nobr>社会</nobr> | 监管不力 | unenforced supervision and regulation |
| <nobr>社会</nobr> | 媒体引导宣传不足 | inadequate advertisement from social media |

### （五）1.3 尾段高分布局（两类模板）

**结尾段常用框架**：① **建议措施** ② **总结概括**（按中间段走"原因/利弊"还是"意义"来选）。

#### 一、建议措施模板（对应负面现象 / 利弊话题）

1. **From what has been discussed above, it is urgent to take some immediate and effective measures.**（引出建议）
2. **In the first place, we are expected to raise the awareness of the significance of 主题.**（建议①：提高意识）
3. **In the second place, more rules are supposed to be made and carried out by the government to restrain people from doing sth and favorable policies are needed to encourage more people to engage in sth.**（建议②：立法约束 + 政策鼓励）
4. **Only in this way can we 展望未来.**（收尾）

**"展望未来"三选一**：

- **enjoy a promising future**（享受美好未来）
- **live in harmony with nature / others**（与自然/他人和谐相处）
- **succeed in maintaining sustainability**（成功保持可持续性）

> **技巧**：建议句一律"**建议角度 + 目的**"成对写——`扩大宣传 → 增强意识`、`制定实施更多规则 → 约束`，句子立刻有逻辑而不是堆套话。

#### 二、总结概括模板（对应意义分析 / 抽象主题）

1. **From what has been mentioned above, we may reasonably come to the conclusion that 重申主题的重要性.**
2. **Those with great accomplishments are living proof that it is 主题 that matters a lot in realization of dreams at every stage of individual life.**

> **技巧**：② 是**强调句**（`it is 主题 that matters a lot`）+ `living proof`（活生生的证明），比 `It is important` 有分量得多；把 `individual life` 扩成 `individual life or the whole process of the world advancement` 即可拔高到人类层面。

### （六）实战演练一：抽象主题（选课系统 → 创新）

**漫画**：一名学生坐在电脑前操作"选课系统"；左侧气泡"知识新　有创新　有难度……"，右侧气泡"给分高　易通过　作业少……"。

**主题/论点**：年轻人要追求创新，不断学习新知识，**要走出舒适区、勇于挑战**。

#### 一、Para.1 描述要点 → 框架填充

- **（1）总体概括**：一个大学生在选课系统中选课。
- **（2）细节描述**：他在两种类型的课程如何选择方面纠结。
    - **"纠结"词块**：do not know / have no idea about sth / be confused about sth.

1. **As is vividly depicted in the picture, a college student is selecting a course from the system.**
2. **The most striking feature is that he is quite confused about which kind to choose: the one that is easy to pass with less homework but a high score or the difficult one with new knowledge and innovation.**
3. **There is no doubt that its intended meaning subtly conveyed should be given a deep consideration.**

**【仿写】**（同模板换题材：毕业岔路口）

> ① **As is vividly depicted in the picture, a group / number of college students are standing on a road.** ② **The most striking feature is that they are quite confused about which road to choose: seeking jobs, pursuing a master degree, going abroad, or starting business on their own.**

#### 二、Para.2 框架填充（意义分析）

- **框架选择**：本主题偏抽象概念 → 走**意义分析**（若讨论"双刃剑"式事物则换利弊分析）。

1. **【表达 2】Without any doubt, the purpose of the cartoon is to show us that young men are supposed to be innovative and persist in learning new knowledge. And it is also hinted that the young need to walk out of the comfort zone and be courageous to meet challenges.**（点明主题）
2. **First and foremost, it is universally held that innovation contributes a lot to individual growth. For example, with this ability, we are more likely to grasp opportunities.**（意义一 · 个人）
3. **In the second place, we all share an idea that innovation also plays a vital role in social progress, which can be easily demonstrated by the fact that almost every social reform results from personal creativity.**（意义二 · 社会）
4. **Last but not least, according to some research findings, by acquiring new knowledge persistently, there is a higher possibility that we could make significant breakthroughs in science and technology so as to efficiently and effectively solve problems that humans are faced with.**（意义三 · 科技/人类）

> **注意**：`with this ability, we are more likely to grasp opportunities` 是把抽象主题词（innovation）**具象成"能力"**再谈好处——抽象主题必须这样落地，否则整段空转。

#### 三、Para.3 总结评论（与 Para.2 保持一致性）

1. **From what has been mentioned above, we may reasonably come to the conclusion that innovation does bring us various benefits and it is now one of the most important spirits in our development.**
2. **Those with great accomplishments are living proof that it is innovation that matters a lot in realization of dreams at every stage of individual life or in the whole process of the world advancement.**

### （七）实战演练二：负向现象（湖上观光 → 环保）

**漫画**：湖面上一艘小船载着数名游客（有人撑篙），湖面漂浮大量垃圾（塑料瓶、易拉罐等）。**标题「旅程之"余"」，判为负向现象。**

#### 一、Para.1 描述要点 → 框架填充

- **（1）总体概括**：两名游客坐船观光游览。
    - **"观光游览"词块**：**sightsee / tour around / enjoy scenery**
- **（2）细节描述**：① 他们在向湖中扔垃圾。② 湖面漂着很多垃圾。

1. **As is clearly depicted in the picture, on a lake two tourists are sightseeing on a boat.**
2. **The most striking feature is that they are throwing rubbish casually into the lake, on which there has been already a lot of disgusting litter.**
3. **There is no doubt that its intended meaning subtly conveyed should be given a deep consideration.**

> **技巧**：② 用 `on which there has been already a lot of ...` 把"扔垃圾"和"湖面已有垃圾"**并进一句**（介词 + which 定从），细节句就写满了。

#### 二、Para.2 框架填充（原因分析模板）

1. **We are supposed to place our attention on the obvious problem revealed in the cartoon: some people ignore the protection of our environment.**（点明问题）
2. **Why does this phenomenon appear? Several possible reasons for it can be figured out.**（设问 + 总述）
3. **First and foremost, the phenomenon may be closely related to the fact that some people are essentially selfish without consideration of others' interests.**（内因 · 自私自利）
4. **Furthermore, inadequate advertisement from social media can also contribute to some extent to unpleasant manners in public.**（外因 · 媒体宣传不足）
5. **Last but not least, it is speculated that perhaps the current situation can be ascribed to unenforced supervision and regulation.**（外因 · 监管不力）

> **技巧**：三条原因分别落在**个人素质 → 媒体宣传 → 监管制度**，正是布局表里"内因 + 外因"的标准配比，也对应（四）五的维度表。

#### 三、Para.3 提出建议措施（与 Para.2 保持一致性）

1. **From what has been discussed above, it is urgent to take some immediate and effective measures.**（引出建议）
2. **On one hand, it is advised to continue to expand promotions in communities and schools so as to bring people into realization of the importance and instancy of protecting environment.**（建议①：扩大宣传 → 增强意识）
3. **On the other hand, more rules are expected to be made and carried out by authorities concerned to restrain environmental damage.**（建议②：有关部门立规 → 约束）
4. **People must work in joint efforts to create sustainable surroundings.**（号召 / 展望未来）

> **技巧**：建议两条正好与 Para.2 的原因**一一对应**——媒体宣传不足 → 扩大宣传；监管不力 → 出台并执行规则。**"与 para.2 保持一致性"是尾段的评分点**。

### （八）本讲总结

```

│   └─ 两幅图：As the picture here conspicuously depicts… → In the first drawing…, By contrast, in the second picture… → We are informed that…

│   ├─ 原因分析：①引出问题 ②Why does this phenomenon appear? ③Several possible reasons… ④内因 ⑤外因一 ⑥外因二
│   ├─ 意义分析：②个人（contributes a lot to）③社会（plays a vital role in）④科技/人类（by doing sth, there is a high possibility…）
│   └─ 利弊分析：①引言（cannot tell whether…unless we properly take advantage of it）②弊 ③个人角度 ④社会角度

│   └─ 总结概括：①come to the conclusion that… ②it is 主题 that matters a lot（living proof）

```
