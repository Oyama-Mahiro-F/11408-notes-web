<h2 align="center">论述型作文·图表作文高分速成</h2>

> **本讲核心**：图表作文速成 = **先判数据型，再套模板**。第一段看**数据类型**（**动态趋势** or **静态比例**）选模板；第二段看**话题类型**（消费生活 / 科技产品 / 个人发展）选论述思路；第三段总结评论、预测未来。全讲给：① 三段时间分配 ② 三段布局表 ③ 各段可填空模板 ④ 一类图表实战演练（2008/2009 国内轿车市场份额）。
> **与相邻笔记的分工**：本文是**图表作文的速成模板**；图画作文用 `论述型作文-图画作文速成.md`；通用三段骨架与范文见 `论述型作文-三段写作模板.md`；全部可替换句式见 `写作句式.md`。

### （一）速成总流程与三段时间分配

1. **审题立意**（2 分钟）
2. **编列提纲**（3 分钟）
3. **布局 + 成文**（30 分钟）

**考场优化步骤**（布局与写作框架已提前背熟）：

```
布局 & 写作框架已定（背模板）
→ 审题立意 → 编列提纲
→ 套用框架成文
```

**审题立意四步**：

1. **判数据类型**——服务首段：**动态趋势 or 静态比例**
2. **标题定主题，数据找规律 / 抓典型** → 概括数据所反映的现象或问题——服务二段 / 三段
3. **定分析角度**（第二段要写哪 2-3 条）
4. 代入英文框架，**填充成文**

### （二）三段高分布局（句数与字数）

| <nobr>段落</nobr> | <nobr>句数</nobr> | <nobr>字数</nobr> | <nobr>写作内容</nobr> |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| <nobr>起始段</nobr> | 3-4 | 40-50 | Part1 如图所示，概述图表<br>Part2 细节描写，**趋势规律 / 典型数据** |
| <nobr>主体段</nobr> | 5-8 | 80-100 | 围绕现象，**分析因 / 果** |
| <nobr>结尾段</nobr> | 2-3 | 30-40 | **总结评论，预测未来** |

> **与图画作文的唯一大差别**：首段不描画面而**读数**（趋势/比例），且必须**抓一个典型数据**（最大、最高、转折）作为亮点。

### （三）第一段速成框架（先判数据型，再选模板）

#### 一、通用句式（两类数据通用）

1. **The chart presents information about / differences between / results of an investigation on ...**（`presents` 可换 **shows**）
2. **To be specific, ......**（细节展开）
3. **And it can be noticed that ....**（抓典型）

#### 二、趋势变化类（动态数据）

1. **The chart presents results of an investigation on the significant changes in terms of the number / percentage of 主体 in China / a city / a college during the time period from xx to xx.**
2. **To be specific, the past X years witnessed a remarkable increase in the number / percentage of 主体1 from approximately xx to nearly xx.**
3. **Meanwhile, there was a dramatic growth in the number / percentage of 主体2 from about xx to around xx.**

#### 三、比例构成类（静态数据）

**【框架 1】逐项排列**

1. **The chart clearly presents different proportions concerning ______.**
2. **As can be seen, the proportion of ______ is __%, being the highest.**（`being the highest` 分词作状语，突出最高项）
3. **And ______ account(s) for __%, followed by ______ with a proportion of __%.**

**【框架 2】合并同类项，突出某个要素**

1. **The chart clearly presents different proportions concerning ______.**
2. **As can be seen, the proportions of ______, ______ and ______ are __%, __% and __% respectively.**
3. **What is noticeable is that ______ account(s) for __%.**

> **选择口径**：先看横轴是**时间**还是**类别**——有时间轴、数据有升降 → **趋势变化类**；一张饼图/一张表把整体分成几块 → **比例构成类**（项目多且想突出某一块时用框架 2 合并同类项）。

### （四）第二段速成框架（两类）

#### 一、【框架 1】分析现象原因

1. **What contributes to the great rise / decline / change in ... / the above trend / the phenomenon reflected in the chart?**（设问起手）
2. **As far as I am concerned, three possible factors can be figured out.**（表态 + 总述；数量写 2-3 个）
3. **First and foremost, the phenomenon may be closely related to the fact that + 句子.**（原因一）
4. **Furthermore, 原因二 can also contribute to some extent to 主题.**（原因二）
5. **Last but not least, it is speculated that perhaps the current situation can be ascribed to 原因三.**（原因三）

> **要点**：① 因果推导句式 ② 原因角度——**三条原因之间要有逻辑（内因 → 外因 / 质量 → 观念 → 价格）**，讲义原话批注"**注意内容逻辑通顺**"。
> **角度具体化**：每条原因后面接一句 `To be specific, / For example, ...` 把抽象原因落到事实上（见（七）第三段式）。

#### 二、【框架 2】利弊分析

1. **The changes concerning 图表核心内容 bring about negative (/ positive) influences on both personal lives and national development.**
2. **Judging from the personal perspective, 影响一.**
3. **Judging from the social angle, 影响二.**
4. **All in all, such changes do greater benefits (/ disadvantages) to both individuals and the whole nation.**

> **选择口径**：图表反映的是**值得解释的单一趋势** → 框架 1（原因）；图表反映的是**中性事物的变化**（如 AI、网购占比上升）→ 框架 2（利弊）。

### （五）第三段速成框架（总结评论 / 提建议）

1. **To sum up, / In conclusion, the trend will continue in the years ahead.**（总结 + 预测）
2. **And ... should ... to ...**（建议 + **目的**；`should ... to ...` 中 to 引出目的）
3. **（Were ... to do sth., ... would surely ....）**（**虚拟语气倒装**，最书面的展望）

**实战版（轿车话题）**：

1. **In conclusion, as the independent research into our domestic cars goes further, it can be predicted that their market share would continue to increase.**
2. **But still there should be more advantages in national cars to make them internationally recognized.**

> **技巧**：`it can be predicted that ... would ...` 是"预测未来"最省力的句式；`Were ... to do sth., ... would surely ...` 是它的亮点版（省略 if 的倒装虚拟）。

### （六）话题类型 → 论述思路（小结三链条）

#### 一、消费生活

1. **社会进步** → 人们的**观念**（idea / view / notion）/**需求**（need / demand）**发生转变**（change / shift）→
2. 现在人们更**重视**（**pay more attention to / place more emphasis on**）更需要 sth / 更**愿意**（**be more willing**）to do sth.

#### 二、科技产品

1. **经济发展** → **科技进步** → 产品更**先进**（advanced）/**丰富多样**（diverse）/**功能多**（multi-functional）/**质量高**（high quality）→
2. 给人们带来了极大的**便利**（convenience）/ **满足人们需求**（**meet one's need / cater to the desire to do sth.**；课上补充"迎合"）

#### 三、个人发展

1. **背景原因**：**竞争激烈**（fierce / intense competition）、**挑战多**（multiple challenges）、**压力大**（overwhelming stress）→
2. **现象**：个人做某事 →
3. **对个人发展的意义**：**增长知识**（acquire more knowledge）/ **积累经验**（accumulate experience）/ **开阔视野**（broaden one's horizon）/ **提升能力**（enhance capacities）→ **提升竞争力**（promote competitiveness）→ **获得更多的机会**（obtain more opportunities）→ **实现更好的个人发展**（achieve better personal growth）

> **用法**：这三条链是**第二段"分析角度"的现成骨架**——先用链条定"写哪几条"，再把每条翻译成英文（句式从 `写作句式.md` 功能四取）；素材页 `写作素材.md`（十四）另存一份词块版备查。

### （七）实战演练：2008 / 2009 国内轿车市场部分品牌份额

#### 一、图表与读数

**标题**：2008、2009 年国内轿车市场部分品牌份额示意图
**纵轴**：份额 0%~40%（每 5% 一档）｜**横轴**：2008 年 / 2009 年｜**图例**：国产品牌、日系品牌、美系品牌

| <nobr>品牌</nobr> | <nobr>2008 年</nobr> | <nobr>2009 年</nobr> | <nobr>趋势</nobr> |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| <nobr>国产品牌</nobr> | 25% | 约 33%（文案写 nearly 35%） | **上升** |
| <nobr>日系品牌</nobr> | 35% | 约 25% | **下降** |
| <nobr>美系品牌</nobr> | 约 15% | 约 15% | **稳定** |

> **读数注意**：柱子本身**没有数据标签**，数值只能按纵轴刻度目测；写法上取整表述更稳（**from 35% to about 25%**、**remained stable at 15%**、**from 25% to nearly 35%**），不要在作文里编造精确数字。

#### 二、审题立意（本题四步）

1. **数据类型**：**动态趋势变化类** → 用 Para.1 的**趋势变化类**框架
2. **具体趋势**：日系份额下降，美系份额稳定，**而国产品牌份额增加**
3. **典型数据**：**2009 年国产品牌份额超过日系品牌**（抓典型）
4. **分析现象**：为什么国产品牌份额增加（**赶超日系？**）→ 服务第二段

**第二段三个分析角度**（先中文想清，再套句）：
1. 随着中国经济发展、汽车产业技术进步，国产汽车各方面质量提高（外观、性能、安全性等）
2. 在爱国精神的驱动下，人们改变了传统观念、提高了对国产汽车的信心，因此越来越多的人愿意购买国产车
3. 国产车的价格优势

#### 三、首段填充

1. **The chart presents the results of an investigation on the market shares of domestic cars in domestic markets between 2008 and 2009.**
    - 词块：**市场份额 = the proportion of ... in the market**
    - 课上订正：原句 `on the market shares of **different car brands**` 中的 `different car brands` 被划掉——**划掉后句子缺宾语**，考场写成 `the market shares of domestic and Japanese cars` 或保留 `different car brands`。
2. **To be specific, the past two years witnessed a dramatic decline in the market share of Japanese cars from 35% to about 25%, and that of American cars remained stable at 15%.**
3. **On the contrary, the percentage of home-made cars increased greatly from 25% to nearly 35%.**（或 **there was a great increase in the percentage of home-made cars from 25% to nearly 35%.**）
4. **And it can be noticed that the share of domestic cars caught up with and surpassed that of Japanese cars in 2009.**

**中文占位版**（先写中文、再逐句译，最省时）：

> The chart presents the results of an investigation on **2008 和 2009 年三种品牌轿车在国内市场的份额**。To be specific, **日系市场份额下降，美系市场份额稳定，而国产品牌市场份额增加**。And it can be noticed that **2009 年国产品牌市场份额赶超日系品牌的市场份额**。

#### 四、第二段填充

1. **What contributes to the above trend reflected in the chart?**
2. **As far as I am concerned, three possible factors can be figured out.**
3. **First and foremost, the phenomenon may be closely related to the fact that, under the driving force of / along with Chinese prospering economy, technologies in the car industry are undergoing dramatic advancement, which contributes to the improvement of national cars in various aspects, such as appearance, performance, security and so on.**
4. **Furthermore, provoked / stimulated / driven by patriotism, the public have changed their traditional views and their confidence in domestic cars has been boosted, which can also contribute to some extent to the growing willingness from people to buy our own cars.**
5. **Last but not least, it is speculated that perhaps the current situation can be ascribed to the advantage of price for home-made cars. To be specific, the price of domestic cars is universally lower than that of foreign cars, which meets the need of average families in China.**

> **注意**：③④⑤ 每条都是"**模板句 + 具体化**"两层——先写英文框架（`the phenomenon may be closely related to the fact that ...`），再用 `which contributes to ...` / `To be specific, ...` 把中文角度落成事实。
> **易错点**：`technologies in car industry **are** undergoing dramatic advancement`——主语 technologies 是复数，谓语用 **are**（讲义原文作 is，属笔误）。

#### 五、第三段填充

1. **In conclusion, as the independent research into our domestic cars goes further, it can be predicted that their market share would continue to increase.**
2. **But still there should be more advantages in national cars to make them internationally recognized.**

#### 六、完整装配稿（按讲义逐句拼合，约 279 词；考场按布局表删减到 160~190 词）

> The chart presents the results of an investigation on the market shares of different car brands in the domestic market between 2008 and 2009. To be specific, the past two years witnessed a dramatic decline in the market share of Japanese cars from 35% to about 25%, and that of American cars remained stable at 15%. On the contrary, the percentage of home-made cars increased greatly from 25% to nearly 35%. And it can be noticed that the share of domestic cars caught up with and surpassed that of Japanese cars in 2009.
>
> What contributes to the above trend reflected in the chart? As far as I am concerned, three possible factors can be figured out. First and foremost, the phenomenon may be closely related to the fact that, along with Chinese prospering economy, technologies in the car industry are undergoing dramatic advancement, which contributes to the improvement of national cars in various aspects, such as appearance, performance, security and so on. Furthermore, provoked by patriotism, the public have changed their traditional views and their confidence in domestic cars has been boosted, which can also contribute to some extent to the growing willingness from people to buy our own cars. Last but not least, it is speculated that perhaps the current situation can be ascribed to the advantage of price for home-made cars. To be specific, the price of domestic cars is universally lower than that of foreign cars, which meets the need of average families in China.
>
> In conclusion, as the independent research into our domestic cars goes further, it can be predicted that their market share would continue to increase. But still there should be more advantages in national cars to make them internationally recognized.

> **用法**：装配稿用于看清"**中文角度 → 英文框架 → 具体化**"的完整链路；考场按（二）布局表控制句数（首段 3-4 句、主体段 5-8 句、尾段 2-3 句）。

### （八）图表作文 ↔ 图画作文（速成讲对照）

| <nobr>对比项</nobr> | <nobr>图表作文</nobr> | <nobr>图画作文</nobr> |

| 共用部分 | 三段时间分配、三段布局、原因三连（First and foremost / Furthermore / Last but not least）、利弊分析、建议与总结模板 | 同左 |
