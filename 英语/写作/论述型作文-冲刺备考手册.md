<h2 align="center">论述型作文·冲刺备考手册（15 页版）</h2>

> **用法**：全篇 **15 节 ＝ 15 页**，每节末尾有分页符（Typora 导出 PDF 即一节一页）。**第 3～8 页是"按段落位置"的模板，第 9～11 页是"按功能"的扩展句式，第 13～15 页是五篇范文**——句式后的例句尽量直接引自范文，并标注出处（如"范文二 ④"＝第 2 篇范文第 4 句），背句式时对着范文一起背。
> **来源**：由 论述型作文-图画作文首段.md、论述型作文-主体段与结论段.md、论述型作文-图画作文速成.md、论述型作文-图表作文速成.md、论述型作文-三段写作模板.md、写作句式.md、写作素材.md 压缩而成。

### 第 1 页 · 三段骨架与时间分配

**三段时间**：审题立意 **2 分钟** → 编列提纲 **3 分钟** → 布局 + 成文 **30 分钟**。框架提前背熟，考场只做三件事：**审题、选框架、填主题词**。

| <nobr>段落</nobr> | <nobr>句数</nobr> | <nobr>字数</nobr> | <nobr>任务</nobr> | <nobr>开篇首句</nobr> |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| <nobr>第一段</nobr> | 3-4 | 40-50 | 描述 + 点题 | As is vividly depicted in the picture, ... ／ The chart presents ... |
| <nobr>第二段</nobr> | 5-8 | 80-100 | 只论证 | First and foremost ... ／ Furthermore ... ／ Last but not least ... |
| <nobr>第三段</nobr> | 2-3 | 30-40 | 只收束 | From what has been discussed above, ... ／ In conclusion, ... |

**三条死规矩**：

1. **首段只描述 + 点题**，不写建议。
2. **第二段是唯一必须写满的段落**（80-100 词），首尾两段靠模板顶住。
3. 全文 **160-200 词**：写不满先补数据或举例，写超了先删首段的细节句。

**考前三天怎么用**：

1. 第一天：通读第 1、2 页，把三段骨架与审题三问背下来。
2. 第二天：背第 3～8 页的每段模板句，配第 9～11 页的扩展句式。
3. 第三天：默写第 13～15 页的五篇范文，再扫第 12 页的十处错点。

### 第 2 页 · 审题三问与模板分流

```
审题三问
├─ 问1 是图画还是图表？
│   ├─ 图画：单主体 → 首段三句版
│   │        双主体 / 双图 → 四句框架 或 两幅图模板
│   └─ 图表：动态趋势（有时间轴、有升降）→ 趋势变化类模板
│            静态比例（一张饼 / 一张表分几块）→ 比例构成类模板
├─ 问2 话题什么性质？
│   ├─ 正面（好品质·好现象）→ 第二段【意义分析】
│   ├─ 负面（不良风气·社会问题）→ 第二段【原因分析】
│   └─ 中性事物（AI·网络·手机）→ 第二段【利弊分析】
└─ 问3 落点在哪个层面？（定尾段）
    ├─ 个人成长 → 意义价值类·微观个人
    ├─ 社会层面（环境·文化·经济）→ 意义价值类·宏观社会
    └─ 具体问题待解决 → 问题解决类（提高意识 + 诉诸立法）
```

**两个判据**：

1. **褒贬判据**：画中人被赞扬 → 走意义；现象被讽刺 → 走原因；事物有两面 → 走利弊。
2. **图表判据**：横轴是**时间** → 趋势类；横轴是**类别** → 比例类。

**定完三问，立刻在草稿纸写下三行**：

1. 主题词（英文，如 optimism / innovation / environmental protection）。
2. 首段要报的两个数据或两处细节。
3. 第二段三条角度的中文关键词（如"质量提高 / 观念转变 / 价格优势"）。

**模板分流速查**：

| <nobr>题目类型</nobr> | <nobr>首段用</nobr> | <nobr>中段用</nobr> | <nobr>尾段用</nobr> |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| <nobr>图画·单主体</nobr> | 第 3 页 一幅图三句版 | 意义 / 原因 / 利弊 | 第 8 页 总结或建议 |
| <nobr>图画·双主体</nobr> | 第 3 页 双主体总述 + 对比衔接 | 意义 / 原因 / 利弊 | 第 8 页 总结或建议 |
| <nobr>图表·趋势</nobr> | 第 4 页 趋势变化类 | 第 5 页 原因分析 | 第 8 页 图表预测 |
| <nobr>图表·比例</nobr> | 第 4 页 比例构成类 | 第 5 页 原因分析 | 第 8 页 图表预测 |

### 第 3 页 · 第一段模板（图画作文）

**① 图画总述**

1. **As is vividly depicted in the picture, 主体 is / are doing sth.**（As 定语从句指代整句 · 句首即亮点）
    例（范文一 ①）：As is vividly depicted in the picture, two men show different attitudes when they see a bottle lying on the ground with water flowing out.
2. **As is clearly depicted in the picture, ...**（同式变体，副词可换 vividly / clearly）
    例（范文四 ①）：As is clearly depicted in the picture, on a lake two tourists are sightseeing on a boat.

**② 细节描述**

3. **The most striking feature is that + 句子**（主系表 + 表语从句 · "最显著的特点是"）
    例（范文四 ②）：The most striking feature is that they are throwing rubbish casually into the lake, on which there has been already a lot of disgusting litter.
4. **Noticeably, 主体 + 动作**（过渡副词 · 由总述转入细节）
    例（范文一 ②）：Noticeably, a man covers his face with a hand and complains "All is gone!".
5. **In contrast, ...**（对比衔接 · 双主体题的第二句）
    例（范文一 ③）：In contrast, the other man says excitedly that luckily there is still a little water, reaching for the bottle.
6. **..., 分词（表伴随）**（非谓语 · 主句写主要动作，次要动作降级）
    例（范文一 ③）：句尾的 reaching for the bottle。

**③ 引向主题（首段第 3 句）**

7. **There is no doubt that its symbolic / intended meaning subtly conveyed should be given a deep consideration.**（形式主语 + 主语从句 + 分词后置定语 · 由画面转向主题）
    例（范文四 ③）：There is no doubt that its intended meaning subtly conveyed should be given a deep consideration.
8. **Just as the title goes, "..."**（引述 · 图画文字说明）
    例（范文一 ④）：Just as the title goes, "Attitude is everything.".

**④ 备用句式**

9. **Depicted / Shown in the picture is / are + 主体**（完全倒装 · 比 The picture depicts 更书面）
    例（补充例句）：Depicted in the picture is an American girl with smile and in Chinese traditional costumes.
10. **In the first drawing, ... . By contrast, in the second picture, ... .**（两图对照 · 双图题专用）
    例（补充例句）：In the first drawing, the flower is in full bloom under the protection of the greenhouse; by contrast, in the second picture, it withers in the storm.
11. **We are informed that + 文字说明**（被动 · 两幅图模板第 3 句）
    例（补充例句）：We are informed that love is like a lamp, which is brighter in darker places.

> **首段注意**：图画作文首段**只描述 + 点题**，2-4 句即可；用词求"有色彩"（complain 代替 say、rush 代替 run、wither 代替 die），句法求"整合分句"（with 复合结构、分词状语）。

### 第 4 页 · 第一段模板（图表作文）

**① 判型**：横轴是时间、有升降 → 趋势变化类；一张饼 / 一张表分成几块 → 比例构成类。

**② 趋势变化类**

1. **The chart presents information about / differences between / results of an investigation on ...**（图表总述通用式，presents 可换 shows）
    例（范文三 ①）：The chart presents the results of an investigation on the market shares of different car brands in the domestic market between 2008 and 2009.
2. **The chart presents results of an investigation on the significant changes in terms of the number / percentage of 主体 in ... during the time period from xx to xx.**（趋势变化类总述句）
    例（补充例句）：The chart presents results of an investigation on the significant changes in terms of the percentage of students studying abroad during the time period from 2015 to 2024.
3. **To be specific, the past X years witnessed a remarkable increase in ... from approximately xx to nearly xx.**（拟人化主语 · 细节句一）
    例（范文三 ②）：To be specific, the past two years witnessed a dramatic decline in the market share of Japanese cars from 35% to about 25%.
4. **..., and that of ... remained stable at 15%.**（that of 替代重复名词 · 一降一稳并进一句）
    例（范文三 ②）：..., and that of American cars remained stable at 15%.
5. **On the contrary, the percentage of ... increased greatly from 25% to nearly 35%.**（对照转折 · 细节句二）
    例（范文三 ③）：On the contrary, the percentage of home-made cars increased greatly from 25% to nearly 35%.
6. **And it can be noticed that ... caught up with and surpassed ... in 2009.**（抓典型 · 首段收尾必加）
    例（范文三 ④）：And it can be noticed that the share of domestic cars caught up with and surpassed that of Japanese cars in 2009.

**③ 比例构成类**

7. **The chart clearly presents different proportions concerning ______.**（比例总述）
    例（范文五 ①）：The chart clearly presents different proportions concerning the career choices of college graduates.
8. **As can be seen, the proportion of ______ is 45%, being the highest.**（分词作状语 · 突出最高项）
    例（范文五 ②）：As can be seen, the proportion of those who choose to pursue a master degree is 45%, being the highest.
9. **And ______ account for 35%, followed by ______ with a proportion of 20%.**（account for + followed by · 依次排列）
    例（范文五 ③）：And those who seek jobs account for 35%, followed by those who start their own business with a proportion of 20%.
10. **The proportions of A, B and C are 45%, 35% and 20% respectively.**（respectively · 并列报比例）
    例（补充例句）：As can be seen, the proportions of graduates choosing further study, employment and business are 45%, 35% and 20% respectively.
11. **What is noticeable is that ______ account(s) for 20%.**（主语从句 · 合并同类项、突出某个要素）
    例（范文五 ④）：What is noticeable is that the percentage of graduates starting their own business has doubled in the past five years.

**④ 词块**：市场份额 = **the proportion of ... in the market**（例：范文三 ① 的 market shares of different car brands）。

> **图表三戒**：只报趋势与典型、不逐项报数；柱子无数据标签时按刻度目测并取整（from 35% to about 25%）；一升一降用 and that of ... remained stable 并进一句。

### 第 5 页 · 第二段模板（一）主题句与原因分析

**① 主题句三式（点明主题）**

1. **Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a problem / topic much concerned in our current life that + 句子.**（同位语从句 · 图画题最常用）
    例（范文二 ②）：Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a social problem much concerned in our current life that some adult children try to shun the responsibility of supporting their aged parents.
2. **Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a topic much concerned in our current life—the significance of 主题词.**（破折号同位语 · 抽象主题用）
    例（范文一 ⑤）：... much concerned in our current life—the significance of an optimistic mindset.
3. **We are supposed to place our attention on the obvious problem revealed in the cartoon: 现象.**（图表 / 图画点问题）
    例（范文四 ④）：We are supposed to place our attention on the obvious problem revealed in the cartoon: some people ignore the protection of our environment.

**② 原因段的四步骨架**

4. **What contributes to the above trend reflected in the chart?**（设问 · 引出原因）
    例（范文三 ⑤）：What contributes to the above trend reflected in the chart?
5. **As far as I am concerned, two / three possible factors can be figured out.**（表态 + 总述）
    例（范文三 ⑥）：As far as I am concerned, two possible factors can be figured out.
6. **The reasons for the phenomenon may be complicated, but we can, to a large extent, ascribe it to the following interrelated factors.**（插入语 · 归因总领 · 图画题常用）
    例（范文二 ③）：The reasons for the phenomenon may be complicated, but we can, to a large extent, ascribe it to the following interrelated factors.
7. **Why does this phenomenon appear? Several possible reasons for it can be figured out.**（设问 + 总述 · 两式合写）
    例（范文四 ⑤）：Why does this phenomenon appear? Several possible reasons for it can be figured out.

**③ 三条原因（内因 → 外因）**

8. **First and foremost, the phenomenon may be closely related to the fact that + 句子.**（原因一 · 内因）
    例（范文四 ⑥）：First and foremost, the phenomenon may be closely related to the fact that some people are essentially selfish without consideration of others' interests.
9. **Furthermore, ... can also contribute to some extent to ...**（原因二 · 外因）
    例（范文四 ⑦）：Furthermore, inadequate advertisement from social media can also contribute to some extent to unpleasant manners in public.
10. **Last but not least, it is speculated that perhaps the current situation can be ascribed to ...**（原因三 · 外因）
    例（范文四 ⑧）：Last but not least, it is speculated that perhaps the current situation can be ascribed to unenforced supervision and regulation.
11. **..., which contributes to ...**（非限制性定语从句 · 把原因链收进一句）
    例（范文三 ⑦）：... technologies in the car industry are undergoing dramatic advancement, which contributes to the improvement of national cars in various aspects.
12. **... can be attributed to the absence of people's consciousness of ...**（被动归因 · 意识缺失）
    例（范文二 ④）：... can be attributed to the absence of people's consciousness of traditional value of family in modern life.
13. **Relevant laws and regulations might not have been strictly enforced to prevent people from doing sth.**（法律法规维度）
    例（范文二 ⑤）：Moreover, relevant laws and regulations might not have been strictly enforced to prevent people from shrugging off their social obligations.

> **三条原因的逻辑**：负面图画题走 **意识 → 媒体观念 → 法律**（范文四）；图表题走 **技术质量 → 观念 → 价格**（范文三）。讲义原话：注意内容逻辑通顺。

### 第 6 页 · 第二段模板（二）意义分析与利弊分析

**① 意义分析（正面话题）**

1. **Definitely, no one can deny the significance of 主题词.**（宾语从句 · 意义总领句）
    例（范文一 ⑥）：Definitely, no one can deny the significance of optimism.
2. **It is widely shared that ... can promote both individual growth and the whole social progress.**（形式主语 + 主语从句 · 总领句客观化版）
    例（补充例句）：It is widely shared that reading can promote both individual growth and the whole social progress.
3. **First and foremost, it is universally held that 主题词 contributes a lot to 意义一（个人）.**（意义一 · 个人层面）
    例（补充例句）：First and foremost, it is universally held that innovation contributes a lot to individual growth.
4. **In the second place, we all share an idea that 主题词 also plays a vital role in 意义二（社会）, which can be easily demonstrated by the fact that + 句子.**（意义二 · 社会层面 + 事实证明）
    例（补充例句）：... which can be easily demonstrated by the fact that almost every social reform results from personal creativity.
5. **Last but not least, by doing sth, there is a high possibility that we could 意义三（科技 / 人类）.**（意义三 + 同位语从句）
    例（补充例句）：Last but not least, by acquiring new knowledge persistently, there is a higher possibility that we could make significant breakthroughs in science and technology.
6. **... is one of the most essential qualities that anyone who desires to achieve success should possess.**（定语从句套叠 · 主题词定性）
    例（范文一 ⑦）：First and foremost, optimism is one of the most essential qualities that anyone who desires to achieve success should possess.
7. **In addition, by being optimistic, we can face challenges with confidence, keep sober and figure out effective measures even in desperate situations.**（by doing 方式 + 结果 · 意义具象化）
    例（范文一 ⑧）
8. **In the end, a positive mind will enable us to achieve our objectives more smoothly and realize our dreams more rapidly.**（enable sb to do + 副词修饰 · 意义句公式：主题 + 句式 + 角度 + 修饰）
    例（范文一 ⑨）
9. **With this ability, we are more likely to grasp opportunities.**（with 短语 + be likely to · 把抽象主题具象成"能力"）
    例（补充例句）

**② 利弊分析（中性事物，如 AI、网络、网购）**

10. **The changes concerning 核心内容 bring about negative / positive influences on both personal lives and national development.**（总起）
    例（补充例句）：The changes concerning artificial intelligence bring about both positive and negative influences on both personal lives and national development.
11. **Judging from the personal perspective, 影响一.**（分词状语 · 个人角度）
    例（补充例句）：Judging from the personal perspective, it may lead to the loss of real communication.
12. **Judging from the social angle, the development of sth without supervision is bound to harm social interests and hinder social progress.**（be bound to · 社会角度）
    例（补充例句）
13. **All in all, such changes do greater benefits / disadvantages to both individuals and the whole nation.**（收束表态）
    例（补充例句）

> **意义三条的顺序**：个人 → 社会 → 科技 / 人类，由近及远；每条都自带"证明"手段（For example / demonstrated by the fact that / according to some research findings）。

### 第 7 页 · 第二段模板（三）辅助论证与具体化

**① 举例说明**

1. **Numerous examples can be given, but this / these will suffice.**（引出例子 · 正式）
    例（补充例句）：Numerous examples can be given, but the following one will suffice.
2. **I can think of no better illustration than the following one(s).**（引出例子 · 更有文采）
    例（补充例句）：I can think of no better illustration than the following one.
3. **For instance, ... ／ Or when ..., ...**（举例正句）
    例（补充例句）：For instance, when someone is starving to death, just a little food and water from you may save his life.

**② 数据统计法**

4. **According to the recent survey / data / study by ..., 75.1% of ... do sth.**（给具体百分比最有说服力）
    例（补充例句）：According to the recent survey by the Chinese Academy of Social Science, 75.1% of teenagers in China have at least an idol from film or music stars.
5. **A survey conducted online by a non-governmental organization has shown that roughly / over ...% of ... do sth.**（数据升级版；主语 A survey 用 has shown）
    例（补充例句）：A survey conducted online has shown that over 60% of college students spend more than three hours on their phones every day.
6. **Recent years have witnessed the prevalence of ...**（时代背景起句；Recent years 是复数，用 have witnessed）
    例（补充例句）：Recent years have witnessed the prevalence of online shopping among the young.

**③ 对比论证法（一正一反）**

7. **Optimistic people can always see the upsides of a matter, which brings them a good mood and fruitful rewards.**（正面句 + 非限制性定从表结果）
    例（补充例句）
8. **On the contrary, pessimistic ones tend to keep complaining about misfortunes and difficulties, thus losing confidence and missing chances.**（反面句 + thus doing 点后果）
    例（补充例句）

**④ 角度具体化（把抽象原因落到事实上）**

9. **To be specific, / For example, ..., which meets the need of ...**（具体化 · 图表原因段第 3 步）
    例（范文三 ⑧）：..., which meets the need of average families in China.
10. **..., on which there has been already a lot of disgusting litter.**（介词 + which 定从 · 一句装两个细节）
    例（范文四 ②）：The most striking feature is that they are throwing rubbish casually into the lake, on which there has been already a lot of disgusting litter.

> **使用原则**：辅助论证是"补一句"用的——主体段已有三条展开句时不必再加；字数不足 160 词时优先补数据或举例，且**一篇之内只用一种**，不要举例、数据、对比全上。

### 第 8 页 · 第三段模板（收束）

**① 套话总结（第一层）**

1. **From what has been discussed above, we may safely draw the conclusion that + 句子.**（同位语从句 · 结论段首选）
    例（范文一 ⑩）：From what has been discussed above, we may safely draw the conclusion that we should embrace positive attitudes.
2. **From what has been discussed above, it is urgent to take some immediate and effective measures.**（同式变体 · 引出建议）
    例（范文四 ⑨）：From what has been discussed above, it is urgent to take some immediate and effective measures.
3. **It is high time we placed great emphasis on ...**（虚拟语气，其后用过去式 · 重申主题）
    例（范文二 ⑥）：It is high time we placed great emphasis on the unpleasant phenomenon.
4. **From what has been mentioned above, we may reasonably come to the conclusion that 重申主题的重要性.**（结论段另一套）
    例（补充例句）：From what has been mentioned above, we may reasonably come to the conclusion that innovation is one of the most important spirits in our development.

**② 给建议 / 给态度（第二层）**

5. **As the modern college students, we are supposed to promote this good quality.**（be supposed to · 微观个人）
    例（范文一 ⑪）：As the modern college students, we are supposed to promote this good quality.
6. **To begin with, relevant laws and regulations should be made to severely punish those who shun the responsibility.**（建议① · 立法惩罚）
    例（范文二 ⑦）
7. **In addition, it is essential to cultivate the awareness among the young adults that respecting and supporting the old is always a virtue of Chinese culture.**（建议② · 提高意识）
    例（范文二 ⑧）
8. **On one hand, it is advised to ... so as to ...**（建议句式与目的结合）

**② 高频错点十条**

1. **被动漏 be**：importance should be **attached** to ...
2. **主谓一致**：Recent years **have** witnessed ...；A survey **has** shown ...；technologies ... **are** undergoing ...
3. **主将从现**：If we **get** fully prepared, we **can** ...
4. **Only 倒装**：Only in this way **can we** curb ...（只倒主句，从句不倒）
5. **同位语从句**：that 后必须是**完整句子**（≠ 定语从句）
6. **It is high time that** + **过去式**：that we **placed** ...
7. **比较级表最高级**：it **couldn't be more** helpful **than** to do sth.
8. **although 不与 but 连用**
9. **分词状语主语一致**：不一致就用 with + 名词 + 分词，或独立主格
10. **elder / eldest**：两者比较用 elder，三者以上之最用 **eldest**

**③ 图表三条铁律**：只报趋势与典型；无标签时按刻度取整；三条原因逻辑通顺且每条都具体化。

**④ 格式与卷面**：三段分明、每段 3-8 句；数字用阿拉伯数字（35%、75.1%）；写完留 3 分钟通读一遍。
