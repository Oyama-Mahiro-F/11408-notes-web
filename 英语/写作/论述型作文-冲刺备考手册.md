<h2 align="center">论述型作文·冲刺备考手册（10 页版）</h2>

> **用法**：全篇 **10 节 ＝ 10 页**，每节末尾有分页符（Typora 导出 PDF 即一节一页）。考前三天每天通读一遍：先背（一）骨架、（二）分流，再记（三）～（五）各段模板，考场靠（六）取句式、（七）避错，（八）（九）当范文默写，（十）是最后的救急料。
> **来源**：由 `论述型作文-图画作文首段.md`、`论述型作文-主体段与结论段.md`、`论述型作文-图画作文速成.md`、`论述型作文-图表作文速成.md`、`论述型作文-三段写作模板.md` 及 `写作句式.md`、`写作素材.md` 压缩而成。

### 第 1 页 · 三段骨架与时间分配

**三段时间**：审题立意 **2 分钟** → 编列提纲 **3 分钟** → 布局 + 成文 **30 分钟**。（框架提前背熟，考场只做"审题 + 选框架 + 填主题词"。）

| <nobr>段落</nobr> | <nobr>句数</nobr> | <nobr>字数</nobr> | <nobr>任务</nobr> | <nobr>保底句</nobr> |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| <nobr>第一段</nobr> | 3-4 | 40-50 | 描述 + 点题 | As is vividly depicted in the picture, ... / The chart presents ... |
| <nobr>第二段</nobr> | 5-8 | 80-100 | 只论证 | First and foremost ... / Furthermore ... / Last but not least ... |
| <nobr>第三段</nobr> | 2-3 | 30-40 | 只收束 | From what has been discussed above, ... / In conclusion, ... |

**三条死规矩**：

1. **首段只描述 + 点题**，不写建议；**尾段只收束**，不补新例子。
2. **第二段是唯一必须写满的段落**（80-100 词），其余两段靠模板顶住。
3. 全文 **160-200 词**；写不满就先补"数据/举例"，写超了先删首段细节句。

### 第 2 页 · 审题三问与模板分流

```
审题三问
├─ 问1 是图画还是图表？
│   ├─ 图画：单主体 → 首段 3 句版｜双主体/双图 → 四句框架 或 两幅图模板
│   └─ 图表：动态趋势（有时间轴、有升降）→ 趋势变化类模板
│            静态比例（一张饼/一张表分几块）→ 比例构成类模板
├─ 问2 话题什么性质？
│   ├─ 正面（好品质·好现象）→ 第二段【意义分析】
│   ├─ 负面（不良风气·社会问题）→ 第二段【原因分析】
│   └─ 中性事物（AI·网络·手机）→ 第二段【利弊分析】
└─ 问3 落点在哪个层面？（定尾段）
    ├─ 个人成长 → 意义价值类·微观个人
    ├─ 社会层面（环境·文化·经济）→ 意义价值类·宏观社会
    └─ 具体问题待解决 → 问题解决类（提高意识 + 诉诸立法）
```

**两个判据**：

- **褒贬判据**：画中人被赞扬 → 走意义；现象被讽刺 → 走原因；事物有两面 → 走利弊。
- **图表判据**：横轴是**时间** → 趋势类；横轴是**类别/项目** → 比例类。

**定完三问，立刻在草稿纸写下**：① 主题词（英文）② 首段要报的两个数据/细节 ③ 第二段三条角度的中文关键词。

### 第 3 页 · 第一段模板（描述 + 点题）

**A. 图画作文**

1. **一幅图（三句版）**：① `As is vividly depicted in the picture, 整体描述.` ② `The most striking feature is that 细节描述.` ③ `There is no doubt that its symbolic meaning subtly conveyed should be given a deep consideration.`
2. **两幅图（三句版）**：① `As the picture here conspicuously depicts, 整体描述.` ② `In the first drawing, ... . By contrast, in the second picture, ... .` ③ `We are informed that 文字说明.`
3. **四句框架（细节多时用）**：总体描述 → 细节 1（`Noticeably, / To be specific,`）→ 细节 2（`In contrast, / Similarly,`）→ 文字说明（`The caption reads, "..." / Just as the title goes, "..."`）。
4. **双主体总述**：`As is depicted in the picture, two men show different attitudes when they see ...`

**B. 图表作文**

1. **通用**：① `The chart presents information about / differences between / results of an investigation on ...`（`presents` 可换 **shows**）② `To be specific, ...` ③ `And it can be noticed that ....`
2. **趋势变化类**：① `The chart presents results of an investigation on the significant changes in terms of the number / percentage of 主体 in ... during the time period from xx to xx.` ② `To be specific, the past X years witnessed a remarkable increase in ... from approximately xx to nearly xx.` ③ `Meanwhile, there was a dramatic growth in ... from about xx to around xx.`
3. **比例构成类**：① `The chart clearly presents different proportions concerning ______.` ② `As can be seen, the proportion of ______ is __%, being the highest.` ③ `And ______ account(s) for __%, followed by ______ with a proportion of __%.`（项目多时改用 `the proportions of A, B and C are __%, __% and __% respectively.` + `What is noticeable is that ______ account(s) for __%.`）

**C. 收尾必加一句"抓典型"**

- 图画/图表通用：`And it can be noticed that the share of ... caught up with and surpassed that of ... in 2009.`（最大、最高、转折）

> **图表三戒**：① 只报趋势与典型，不逐项报数；② 柱上无标签时按刻度目测并取整（`from 35% to about 25%`）；③ 一升一降用 `and that of ... remained stable at 15%` 并进一句。

### 第 4 页 · 第二段模板（论证）

**A. 主题句（点明主题，可放首段尾或二段首）**

1. `The symbolic / intended meaning of the picture is to show us that importance should be attached to 主题词.`
2. `Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a problem / topic much concerned in our current life that + 句子.`
3. 图表：`We are supposed to place our attention on the obvious problem revealed in the cartoon / chart: 现象.`

**B. 原因分析（负面话题 + 图表题）**

1. `What contributes to the above trend reflected in the chart?`（设问）
2. `As far as I am concerned, three possible factors can be figured out.`（总述）
3. `First and foremost, the phenomenon may be closely related to the fact that + 句子.`（原因一**内因**）
4. `Furthermore, 原因二 can also contribute to some extent to 主题.`（原因二**外因**）
5. `Last but not least, it is speculated that perhaps the current situation can be ascribed to 原因三.`（原因三**外因**）
6. **具体化**：每条后面补一句 `To be specific, / For example, ... , which contributes to / meets the need of ...`

**C. 意义分析（正面话题）**

1. `Definitely, no one can deny the significance of 主题词.`
2. `First and foremost, it is universally held that 主题词 contributes a lot to 意义一（个人）.`
3. `In the second place, we all share an idea that 主题词 also plays a vital role in 意义二（社会）, which can be easily demonstrated by the fact that + 句子.`
4. `Last but not least, by doing sth, there is a high possibility that we could 意义三（科技/人类）.`
5. 亮点公式：**主题 + 句式 + 角度 + 修饰** —— `A positive mind will enable us to achieve our objectives more smoothly and realize our dreams more rapidly.`

**D. 利弊分析（中性事物）**

1. `The changes concerning 核心内容 bring about negative / positive influences on both personal lives and national development.`
2. `Judging from the personal perspective, 影响一. Judging from the social angle, 影响二.`
3. `All in all, such changes do greater benefits / disadvantages to both individuals and the whole nation.`

**E. 三条辅助论证（选一处嵌入即可）**：举例 `Numerous examples can be given, but this will suffice.` ／ 数据 `According to the recent survey by ..., 75.1% of ... do sth.` ／ 对比 `On the contrary, ... , thus losing confidence.`

> **三条原因的逻辑**：负面题走 **意识 → 利益 → 法律**；图表题走 **质量/技术 → 观念 → 价格**。讲义原话：**注意内容逻辑通顺**。

### 第 5 页 · 第三段模板（收束）

**A. 建议措施型（负面题 / 问题解决类 / 图表题）**

1. `From what has been discussed above, it is urgent to take some immediate and effective measures.`（引出建议）
2. `In the first place, we are expected to raise the awareness of the significance of 主题.`（提高意识）
3. `In the second place, more rules are supposed to be made and carried out by the government to restrain people from doing sth and favorable policies are needed to encourage more people to engage in sth.`（立法 + 政策）
4. 或换成一对一：`On one hand, it is advised to continue to expand promotions in communities and schools so as to bring people into realization of the importance of 主题. On the other hand, more rules are expected to be made and carried out by authorities concerned to restrain sth.`
5. `People must work in joint efforts to create sustainable surroundings.` / `Only in this way can we enjoy a promising future.`（号召 / 展望）

**B. 总结概括型（意义分析 / 抽象主题）**

1. `From what has been mentioned above, we may reasonably come to the conclusion that 重申主题的重要性.`
2. `Those with great accomplishments are living proof that it is 主题 that matters a lot in realization of dreams at every stage of individual life.`（**强调句 + living proof**）

**C. 图表题专用收尾**

1. `To sum up, / In conclusion, the trend will continue in the years ahead.`
2. `In conclusion, as the independent research into ... goes further, it can be predicted that ... would continue to increase.`
3. `But still there should be more advantages in ... to make them internationally recognized.`

**D. 两个亮点收尾式（任选一句）**

1. **虚拟倒装**：`Were we to do sth, we would surely ...`（省略 if，最书面）
2. **部分倒装**：`Only in this way can we curb the current evil phenomenon revealed in the picture.`

> **三层去重**：第一层已"下结论"，第三层就只做展望；第二层已给措施，第三层不再重复措施。**建议必须与第二段的原因一一对应**（宣传不足→扩大宣传；监管不力→出台规则）——讲义原话"**与 para.2 保持一致性**"。

### 第 6 页 · 万能句式速查（按功能）

**一 开篇描述**

1. `As is vividly depicted in the picture, ...` ／ `Depicted in the picture is / are ...`
2. `The symbolic / intended meaning of the picture is to show us that importance should be attached to ...`
3. `Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a problem much concerned in our current life that ...`
4. `The most striking feature is that ...` ／ `There is no doubt that its symbolic meaning subtly conveyed should be given a deep consideration.`
5. `The chart presents information about / differences between / results of an investigation on ...`
6. `As can be seen, the proportion of ______ is __%, being the highest.` ／ `And ______ account(s) for __%, followed by ...`

**二 陈述事实与数据**

7. `To be specific, the past X years witnessed a remarkable increase in ... from ... to ...`
8. `... witnessed a dramatic decline in ... from 35% to about 25%, and that of ... remained stable at 15%.`
9. `On the contrary, the percentage of ... increased greatly from 25% to nearly 35%.`
10. `And it can be noticed that ... caught up with and surpassed ... in 2009.`
11. `According to the recent survey by ..., 75.1% of ... do sth.` ／ `Recent years have witnessed the prevalence of ...`
12. `Numerous examples can be given, but this / these will suffice.`

**三 表达观点与态度**

13. `From my perspective, ...` ／ `As far as ... is concerned, ...`
14. `Definitely, no one can deny the significance of ...` ／ `It is widely shared that ... can promote both individual growth and the whole social progress.`
15. `... is one of the most essential qualities that anyone who desires to achieve success should possess.`
16. `Without any doubt, the purpose of the cartoon is to show us that ... And it is also hinted that ...`

**四 因果论证**

17. `Because + 句子, 主句` ／ `主句, so that + 句子` ／ `so + adj. + that + 句子`
18. `主句, which will ...` ／ `..., which in the long run will hinder ...`
19. `First and foremost, the phenomenon may be closely related to the fact that ...`
20. `Furthermore, ... can also contribute to some extent to ...` ／ `Last but not least, it is speculated that perhaps ... can be ascribed to ...`
21. `..., which can be attributed to the absence of people's consciousness of sth.`
22. `Judging from the social angle, ... is bound to harm social interests and hinder social progress.`

**五 条件与让步 · 六 目的与手段**

23. `If we get fully prepared, we can ...`（**主将从现**）
24. `Although the task is tough, ...` ／ `..., while ...`
25. `so that we can ...` ／ `in order to do sth.` ／ `by doing sth, we can ...`
26. `It is necessary that we (should) take measures ...`（虚拟）

**七 修饰与限定 · 八 强调与倒装**

27. `People who live in a comfortable environment cannot ...` ／ `..., the most important of which is ...`
28. `Only in this way can we ...` ／ `It is constant learning that inspires us to ...`
29. `It is not difficult for us to find that ...`（形式主语，长主语后置）
30. `It couldn't be more helpful than to do sth.` ／ `it is high time that we placed great emphasis on ...`（后接**过去式**）

**九 议论与疑问**

31. `Why does this phenomenon appear? Several possible reasons for it can be figured out.`
32. `We should consider whether we can achieve it.` ／ `we can't help wondering whether ...`

**十 建议、呼吁与收尾**

33. `From what has been discussed above, we may safely draw the conclusion that ...`
34. `In the first place, we are expected to raise the awareness of the significance of ...`
35. `On the one hand ... On the other hand, we should appeal to the authorities to make strict laws ...`
36. `Those with great accomplishments are living proof that it is 主题 that matters a lot ...`
37. `I believe in joint efforts we humans can overcome this difficulty ...`
38. `（Were ... to do sth., ... would surely ....）` ／ `To sum up, the trend will continue in the years ahead.`

### 第 7 页 · 替换词库与高频错点

**A. 替换词库（保底 → 亮点）**

| <nobr>中文</nobr> | <nobr>保底版</nobr> | <nobr>亮点 / 书面版</nobr> |
|:---|:---|:---|
| <nobr>应该</nobr> | should | be supposed to do / be expected to do |
| <nobr>重视</nobr> | pay attention to | attach great importance to / place much emphasis on |
| <nobr>说</nobr> | say | complain / exclaim |
| <nobr>跑</nobr> | run | rush / dash |
| <nobr>死 / 枯</nobr> | die | wither |
| <nobr>重要</nobr> | important | essential / vital / significant |
| <nobr>使能够</nobr> | let sb do | enable / allow / empower sb to do |
| <nobr>有利于</nobr> | be good for | be beneficial to / play a positive role in |
| <nobr>导致</nobr> | cause | lead to / result in / give rise to |
| <nobr>越来越</nobr> | more and more | increasingly |
| <nobr>我认为</nobr> | I think | From my perspective, / As far as ... is concerned, |
| <nobr>满足需求</nobr> | satisfy | meet one's need / cater to the desire to do sth. |
| <nobr>市场份额</nobr> | share | the proportion of ... in the market |

**B. 高频错点十条（写完逐条扫）**

1. **被动漏 be**：`importance should be **attached** to ...`
2. **主谓一致**：`Recent years **have** witnessed ...`；`A survey **has** shown ...`；`technologies ... **are** undergoing ...`
3. **主将从现**：`If we **get** fully prepared, we **can** ...`
4. **Only 倒装**：`Only in this way **can we** curb ...`（只倒主句）
5. **同位语从句**：that 后必须是**完整句子**（≠ 定语从句）
6. **It is high time** + **过去式**：`that we **placed** ...`
7. **比较级表最高级**：`it **couldn't be more** helpful **than** to do sth.`
8. **although 不与 but 连用**
9. **分词状语主语一致**：不一致就用 `with + 名词 + 分词` 或独立主格
10. **elder / eldest**：两者比较用 elder，三者以上之最用 **eldest**

**C. 图表三条铁律**：只报趋势与典型；无标签时按刻度取整；第二段三条原因逻辑通顺并具体化。

### 第 8 页 · 范文一（图画·正面·"半瓶水"乐观题，208 词）

> As is vividly depicted in the picture, two men show different attitudes when they see a bottle lying on the ground with water flowing out. Noticeably, a man covers his face with a hand and complains "All is gone!". In contrast, the other man says excitedly that luckily there is still a little water, reaching for the bottle. Just as the title goes, "Attitude is everything."
>
> Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a topic much concerned in our current life—the significance of an optimistic mindset. Definitely, no one can deny the significance of optimism. First and foremost, optimism is one of the most essential qualities that anyone who desires to achieve success should possess. In addition, by being optimistic, we can face challenges with confidence, keep sober and figure out effective measures even in desperate situations. In the end, a positive mind will enable us to achieve our objectives more smoothly and realize our dreams more rapidly.
>
> From what has been discussed above, we may safely draw the conclusion that we should embrace positive attitudes. As the modern college students, we are supposed to promote this good quality. And I am quite sure that if we can keep optimistic, there would be a promising future awaiting us.

**槽位对照**：

- 首段：总述（As is vividly depicted + 双主体 show different attitudes）→ 细节 1（Noticeably）→ 细节 2（In contrast + 分词 reaching for）→ 文字说明（Just as the title goes）
- 中段：主题句（Undoubtedly ... that）→ 总领句（no one can deny）→ 三条展开（First and foremost / In addition / In the end）
- 尾段：套话（From what has been discussed above）→ 态度（be supposed to）→ 展望（if we can ... , there would be ...）

> **可迁移点**：① 双主体题一律"总述两人态度不同 → 一人一句"；② 品质类主题可用"最重要的品质之一 + 这样做的好处 + enable sb to do 加副词修饰"三段式；③ 尾段不必新意，套话 + 态度 + 展望即可。

### 第 9 页 · 范文二（图画·负面·养老"足球赛"，195 词）与范文三（图表·轿车份额，精简版 202 词）

**范文二（负面话题）**

> As is vividly depicted in the picture, four adult children are standing in front of their own goals, kicking their aged father like a ball.
>
> Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a social problem much concerned in our current life that some adult children try to shun the responsibility of supporting their aged parents. The reasons for the phenomenon may be complicated, but we can, to a large extent, ascribe it to the following interrelated factors. To begin with, this undesirable phenomenon of maltreatment of the elders can be attributed to the absence of people's consciousness of traditional value of family in modern life. Moreover, relevant laws and regulations might not have been strictly enforced to prevent people from shrugging off their social obligations.
>
> It is high time we placed great emphasis on the unpleasant phenomenon. To begin with, relevant laws and regulations should be made to severely punish those who shun the responsibility. In addition, it is essential to cultivate the awareness among the young adults that respecting and supporting the old is always a virtue of Chinese culture. Only in this way can we curb the current evil phenomenon revealed in the picture.

**范文三（图表·趋势变化类，精简版）**

> The chart presents the results of an investigation on the market shares of different car brands in the domestic market between 2008 and 2009. To be specific, the past two years witnessed a dramatic decline in the market share of Japanese cars from 35% to about 25%, and that of American cars remained stable at 15%. On the contrary, the percentage of home-made cars increased greatly from 25% to nearly 35%. And it can be noticed that the share of domestic cars caught up with and surpassed that of Japanese cars in 2009.
>
> What contributes to the above trend reflected in the chart? As far as I am concerned, two possible factors can be figured out. First and foremost, the phenomenon may be closely related to the fact that, along with Chinese prospering economy, technologies in the car industry are undergoing dramatic advancement, which contributes to the improvement of national cars in various aspects, such as appearance, performance and security. Last but not least, it is speculated that perhaps the current situation can be ascribed to the advantage of price for home-made cars, which meets the need of average families in China.
>
> In conclusion, it can be predicted that the market share of our domestic cars would continue to increase.

> **两篇的共通骨架**：负面题 =（首段描图/读数）→（原因总领 + 两条原因）→（建议两条 + Only 倒装收尾）；图表题 =（读数 + 抓典型）→（设问 + 三条原因，每条都具体化）→（预测趋势）。

### 第 10 页 · 素材角度链 + 考场流程 + 交卷前清单

**A. 六大主题的角度骨架（正面题用"意义"，负面题用"归因"）**

| <nobr>主题</nobr> | <nobr>论述角度骨架</nobr> | <nobr>落点</nobr> |
|:---|:---|:---|
| <nobr>文化教育</nobr> | 事物属性 → 宏观意义 → 微观意义 | 态度三条 + 行动两条 |
| <nobr>经济生活</nobr> | 趋势 → 对生活的影响（就业收入·生活水平·消费） | 国家政策 / 科技创新 / 人才培养 |
| <nobr>环境保护</nobr> | 问题归因（内因 · 外因） | 企业社会六条 + 个人两条 |
| <nobr>运动健康</nobr> | 重要性 → 如何保持 / 提升 | 个人习惯 + 休闲放松 + 社会措施 |
| <nobr>人生哲理</nobr> | 品质的重要性 → 如何培养 | 思想意识 + 奋斗行动 |
| <nobr>道德伦理</nobr> | 社会风尚：重要性 ／ 不良风气：归因 + 解决 | 社会发展 + 个人 |

**B. 图表作文三类话题的论述链（第二段直接照搬）**

1. **消费生活**：社会进步 → 观念 / 需求转变 → 人们更重视 / 更愿意做某事
2. **科技产品**：经济发展 → 科技进步 → 产品先进 / 多样 / 多功能 / 高质量 → 生活便利 / 满足需求
3. **个人发展**：竞争激烈·挑战多·压力大 → 个人做某事 → 增长知识 / 积累经验 / 开阔视野 / 提升能力 → 竞争力 → 机会 → 个人发展

**C. 考场 35 分钟流程**

```
0-2min   审题三问（图画/图表 · 正/负/中性 · 落点）+ 写主题词
2-5min   列提纲：首段两个数据/细节 + 第二段三条角度关键词
5-35min  套框架成文：首段 4 句 → 二段 6 句 → 尾段 3 句
35-38min 通读检查（错点十条 + 字数）
```

**D. 交卷前 60 秒清单**

1. 三段任务有没有串（首段写建议？尾段塞新例子？）
2. 第二段有没有**总领句 + 层次词**（First and foremost / Furthermore / Last but not least）
3. 尾段有没有**回扣主题**（重申 / 预测 / 展望）
4. 十处高频错点扫一遍（尤其 `should be attached to`、主谓一致、Only 倒装、high time + 过去式）
5. 字数 160-200；段落数 3 段；字迹分段清楚

> **最后一句**：框架背熟 + 主题词填对 + 三处亮点（一句倒装、一句从句、一个好词），就是稳稳的高分卷。
