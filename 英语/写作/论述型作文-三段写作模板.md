<h2 align="center">论述型作文·三段写作模板</h2>

> **本文定位**：把《论述型作文-图画作文首段.md》与《论述型作文-主体段与结论段.md》两讲压成**一套可直接套用的填空模板**——考场按"填槽"写，不逐句现场原创。
> **用法**：先做（二）审题三问定骨架，再照（三）～（五）填三段；时间紧就背每处的**保底版**，时间够就换**亮点版**。范文见（六），自查见（八）。

### （一）模板总览：三段十二句

| <nobr>段落</nobr> | <nobr>句位</nobr> | <nobr>任务</nobr> | <nobr>保底句式</nobr> | <nobr>升级方向</nobr> |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **第一段** | 第 1 句 | 总体描述 | As is vividly depicted in the picture, 主体 is / are doing sth. | 完全倒装 Depicted / Shown in the picture is / are ... |
| <nobr>第一段</nobr> | 第 2-3 句 | 细节描述 | Noticeably, / To be specific, + 主体动作 | 加修饰语、with 伴随、分词状语 |
| <nobr>第一段</nobr> | 第 4 句 | 文字说明 | The caption reads, "..." | Just as the title goes, "..." |
| **第二段** | 第 1 句 | **主题句** | The symbolic meaning of the picture is to show us that importance should be attached to ... | Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed ... **that + 句子** |
| <nobr>第二段</nobr> | 第 2 句 | **总领句** | 正面：Definitely, no one can deny the significance of ...<br>负面：The reasons ... ascribe it to the following interrelated factors. | It is widely shared that ... can promote both individual growth and the whole social progress. |
| <nobr>第二段</nobr> | 第 3-5 句 | 展开 2-3 条 | 正面 First and foremost / In addition / In the end<br>负面 To begin with / Moreover | 意义句加副词修饰（more smoothly / more rapidly）；归因三角度轮换 |
| <nobr>第二段</nobr> | 第 6 句（可选） | 辅助论证 | For instance, ... | 数据（According to the recent survey ...）/ 对比（On the contrary ...） |
| **第三段** | 第 1 句 | 套话总结 | From what has been discussed above, we may safely draw the conclusion that ... | It is high time that we placed great emphasis on ... |
| <nobr>第三段</nobr> | 第 2 句 | 态度决心或措施 | 个人：I am determined to be more + adj. ...<br>社会：we are supposed to promote ... | we are bound to benefit from ... and are accountable for promoting ...<br>问题解决类：On the one hand ... On the other hand ... |
| <nobr>第三段</nobr> | 第 3 句 | 包装结尾 | I believe in joint efforts we humans can overcome this difficulty ... | Only in this way can we curb the current evil phenomenon revealed in the picture. |

**字数分配**：首段 40~50 词（2-4 句）→ 主体段 70~90 词（5-6 句）→ 结论段 45~60 词（3 句），合计 **160~200 词**。

> **一句话规矩**：**首段只描述 + 点题，主体段只论证，结论段只收束**——三段的活儿不要互相串（首段别写建议、结论段别补新例子）。

### （二）审题三问：定骨架

```
审题三问
├─ 问1 图画类型
│   ├─ 单主体（一人/一物做一事）→ 首段 3 句（总述 + 细节 + 文字说明）
│   ├─ 双主体（两人态度相反）  → 首段 4 句（加 In contrast 分述）
│   └─ 双图（相似/前后对比）    → 首段 4 句（加 Similarly / In contrast 衔接）
├─ 问2 话题性质
│   ├─ 正面（好品质·好现象）→ 主体段走【意义分析】：总领句 + 意义 2-3 条
│   └─ 负面（不良风气·社会问题）→ 主体段走【原因分析】：总领句 + 原因 2-3 条
└─ 问3 落点层面（定结论段第二层）
    ├─ 个人成长（乐观·坚持·独立）→ 意义价值类·微观个人
    ├─ 社会层面（环境·文化·经济）→ 意义价值类·宏观社会
    └─ 具体问题待解决（养老·诚信缺失）→ 问题解决类
```

> **判据**：画中人是**被赞扬的态度** → 正面走意义；画中现象**被讽刺/批判** → 负面走原因。**两套主线不混用**：负面话题硬写"意义"直接跑题。

### （三）第一段模板（描图 + 点题）

#### 一、四句填空骨架

```
第1句 总体描述：As is vividly depicted in the picture, 【主体】 【is / are doing sth】.
第2句 细节描述：【Noticeably / To be specific】, 【主体1：动作 + 修饰】.
第3句 细节描述：【In contrast / Similarly】, 【主体2 / 环境：动作 + 修饰】.
第4句 文字说明：【The caption reads / Just as the title goes】, "【题字】".
```

- **单主体题**：第 3 句可省；或把第 2-3 句合成"加修饰的一句"。
- **可选搭桥句**（想把主题句提前到首段尾时用）：**The intended meaning of the picture is to bring us to a full awareness of 【主题】.**

#### 二、第 1 句五式（按需取用）

1. **As is vividly depicted in the picture / cartoon, 主体 is / are doing sth.**（As 定语从句指代整句 · **首选**，句首即亮点）
    - **As is vividly depicted in the picture**, a young man is rushing with all his effort on the track towards the finishing line.
2. **Depicted / Shown / Presented in the picture is / are + 主体**（**完全倒装** · 最书面；谓语随真正主语变化）
    - **Depicted in the picture is** an American girl with smile and in Chinese traditional costumes.
3. **This (set of) drawing(s) vividly portrays + 主体 + who / which + is / are doing sth.**（主句 + 定语从句 · 单主体总述）
    - **This drawing vividly portrays** a young man **who is rushing** on the track.
4. **Shown in the picture is a (vivid) scene in which + 主体 + is / are doing sth.**（完全倒装 + 定语从句 · 最书面的场景式）
    - **Shown in the picture is a vivid scene in which** a little lamp is giving off bright light.
5. **As is depicted in the picture, 双主体 + show different attitudes / have different responses when they do sth.**（**双主体专用**）
    - **As is depicted in the picture, two men show different attitudes when they see** a bottle lying on the ground with water flowing out.

#### 三、双主体 / 双图的衔接

1. **相似主体**（两图讲同一件事）：**In the first photo, ....** ／ **Similarly / Meanwhile, in the second picture, ....**
2. **对比主体**（两人两图态度相反）：**句 1. In contrast / On the contrary, 句 2.** ／ **句 1, while 句 2.**

#### 四、细节描述三招（"用词多色彩"）

1. **名词前后加修饰**：**an energetic** runner（形容词前置）／**a little lamp with a smiling face**（介词短语后置）
2. **副词、介词短语修饰动词**：say **proudly / frustratedly** ／ The flower is **in full bloom** under the protection of the greenhouse.
3. **换一个更贴切的词**：say frustratedly → **complain**；run towards the finish line → **rush / dash to**；The flower dies. → The flower **withers**.；be reading → **be dedicated to reading**

#### 五、末句文字说明

1. **The caption reads, "..."**（图画下方的题字 / 说明文字）
2. **Just as the title goes, "..."**（借标题引向主题）

> **提示**：题字本身带寓意时，写完文字说明**主体段的主题句顺势承接**，两段就自然衔接（见（四）一）。

### （四）第二段模板（主题句 + 展开论述）

#### 一、第一层：主题句（三式）

1. **The symbolic / intended meaning of the picture is to show us that importance should be attached to + 主题词.**（意义型 · 保底）
    - **The symbolic meaning of the picture is to show us that importance should be attached to** an optimistic mindset.
2. **The picture has (subtly) reflected / revealed the social phenomenon prevalent / common among the public nowadays that + 句子.**（现象型）
3. **Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a problem / topic much concerned in our current life that + 句子.**（**最常用**，社会问题与抽象话题通用）
    - **Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a social problem much concerned in our current life that** some adult children try to **shun** the responsibility of supporting their aged parents.

> **搭桥**：Directions 第 2) 项 explain its intended meaning 的落点是 **para.1 尾 / para.2 首**——首段末句停在"文字说明"，第二段首句用上表任一句点题，两段无缝。

#### 二、第二层 A：正面话题（意义分析）

```
主题句 → 总领句（意义）→ 意义1（First and foremost）→ 意义2（In addition）→ 意义3（In the end，可选）
```

1. **总领句保底**：**Definitely, no one can deny the significance of + 主题词.**
2. **总领句升级**：**It is widely shared that + 主题词 + can promote both individual growth and the whole social progress.**
3. **现成展开句一（主题词定性）**：**... is one of the most essential qualities that anyone who desires to achieve success should possess.**
4. **现成展开句二（方式 + 结果）**：**By being optimistic, we can face challenges with confidence, keep sober and figure out effective measures even in desperate situations.**
5. **现成展开句三（加副词修饰）**：**A positive mind will enable us to achieve our objectives more smoothly and realize our dreams more rapidly.**
    - **意义句公式 = 主题 + 句式 + 角度 + 修饰**（`enable sb to do` + 角度〔目标 / 梦想〕+ `more smoothly / more rapidly`）

**填充角度（没话写时从这里选）**：

| <nobr>角度</nobr> | 可填内容 |
|:---|:---|
| <nobr>宏观维度</nobr> | harmony（和谐）、sustainability（可持续）、challenge（挑战）、difficulty / hardship（困难）、stress / pressure（压力）、efficiency（效率）、effectiveness（效果） |
| <nobr>个人发展</nobr> | ① 获得 / 迎接 / 抓住机遇 ② 面对 / 应对困难、挑战、压力 ③ 又快又好——效率 / 效果 |
| <nobr>配套词块</nobr> | 发展 development / advancement / growth / progress；实现 realize / make ... come true；取得 achieve / accomplish / fulfill / obtain / attain；目标 goal、梦想 dream、成功 success、机遇 opportunity |

#### 三、第二层 B：负面话题（原因分析）

```
主题句 → 总领句（原因）→ 原因1（To begin with）→ 原因2（Moreover）→ 原因3（可选：Finally）
```

1. **总领句保底**：**Several factors, both individual and social, may be responsible for the negative phenomenon.**
2. **总领句升级**：**The reasons for the phenomenon may be complicated, but we can, to a large extent, ascribe it to the following interrelated factors.**（`can, to a large extent,` 是插入语，写出来语气客观克制）
3. **归因句式①意识缺失**：**The phenomenon can be attributed to the absence / lack / insufficiency of people's consciousness / awareness of sth.**
4. **归因句式②利益驱使**：**To gain excessive private profits, sb tend to do sth without much consideration of the rights / interests of sb.**
5. **归因句式③法律法规**：**Relevant laws and regulations might not have been strictly enforced to restrict / prevent people from doing sth.**

**归因维度（三选二即可）**：

| <nobr>层面</nobr> | 常见落点 |
|:---|:---|
| <nobr>意识（主观）</nobr> | 意识不足 / 忽视……、道德缺失、自私自利 |
| <nobr>利益（动机）</nobr> | 为攫取私利而不顾他人权益、缺少自我约束 / 管理 |
| <nobr>制度（外在）</nobr> | 制度不完善、监管不力、媒体引导宣传不足、法规未严格执行 |

> **记忆**：负面话题归因 = **意识 → 利益 → 法律**，任选 2-3 条，段落自然有"从个人到社会"的层次。

#### 四、辅助论证（选一处嵌入，别三段都用）

1. **举例说明**：
    - 引出：**Numerous examples can be given, but this / these will suffice.** ／ **I can think of no better illustration than the following one(s).**
    - 展开：**For instance, ...** ／ **Or when ..., ...**（例子写完回扣主题：**... is just like a lamp in a dark place where light is most needed.**）
2. **数据统计法**：
    - **According to the recent survey / data / figure / statistic / study by the Chinese Academy of Social Science, ...% of sb do sth.**
    - **A survey conducted online by a non-governmental organization has shown that roughly / more than / over ...% of sb do sth.**
    - 起句：**Recent years have witnessed the prevalence of + 现象.**
3. **对比论证法**（正反各一句）：
    - **Optimistic people can always see the upsides of a matter, which brings them a good mood and fruitful rewards.**
    - **On the contrary, pessimistic ones tend to keep complaining about misfortunes and difficulties, thus losing confidence and missing chances.**

> **用法**：辅助论证是"补一句"用的，**主体段已经有三条展开句时不必再加**；字数不够（不足 160 词）时优先补**数据**或**举例**。

### （五）第三段模板（结论三层）

#### 一、三层填空骨架

```
第1层 套话总结：From what has been discussed above, we may safely draw the conclusion that 【重申主题】.
第2层 态度决心或措施：【意义价值类】I am determined to be more 【adj.】 so that ... ／ 【问题解决类】On the one hand ... On the other hand ...
第3层 包装结尾：I believe in joint efforts we humans can overcome this difficulty and we will definitely enjoy a more promising future.
```

#### 二、第一层：套话，总结归纳 / 重申主题

1. **From what has been discussed above, we may safely draw the conclusion that + 句子.**
2. **It is high time that we placed great emphasis on + 名词.**（**虚拟语气**：that 后谓语用**过去式** placed）

#### 三、第二层：态度决心或采取措施

**（1）意义价值类**

1. **微观个人**（个人成长类话题）：
    - **To conclude, for a healthy personal growth, it couldn't be more helpful than to do sth.**（比较级表最高级）
    - **And I am determined to be more + adj. so that there would be a promising future awaiting me.**
2. **宏观社会**（环境资源 / 文化 / 经济类话题）：
    - **As the modern college students, we are bound to benefit from ... and are accountable for promoting ... .**
    - **I am quite sure that if everybody were to ..., there would be a promising future awaiting us.**（虚拟语气 were to / would be）

**（2）问题解决类**（具体问题待解决时）

- **On the one hand, it is essential to enhance / cultivate the awareness among the public that sth is vital for everyone.**
- **On the other hand, we should appeal to the authorities to make strict laws to curb the negative impact of this problem and severely punish those who disobey or break the rules.**

#### 四、第三层：包装结尾，展望未来

1. **I believe in joint efforts we humans can overcome this difficulty and we will definitely enjoy a more promising future.**（共同愿景式）
2. **Only in this way can we curb the current evil phenomenon revealed in the picture.**（**部分倒装**：Only + 状语置句首 → 主句倒装）

> **去重**：第一层已经"下结论"，第三层就不要再下结论；第二层已经"给措施"，第三层只做**展望**。

### （六）三篇完整填空示范（范文）

#### 一、示范一：正面话题·人生哲理（"半瓶水"乐观题，208 词——删去总领句即 199 词）

> As is vividly depicted in the picture, two men show different attitudes when they see a bottle lying on the ground with water flowing out. **Noticeably**, a man covers his face with a hand and complains "All is gone!". **In contrast**, the other man says excitedly that luckily there is still a little water, reaching for the bottle. **Just as the title goes**, "Attitude is everything."
>
> **Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a topic much concerned in our current life—the significance of an optimistic mindset.** **Definitely, no one can deny the significance of optimism.** **First and foremost**, optimism is one of the most essential qualities that anyone who desires to achieve success should possess. **In addition**, by being optimistic, we can face challenges with confidence, keep sober and figure out effective measures even in desperate situations. **In the end**, a positive mind will enable us to achieve our objectives more smoothly and realize our dreams more rapidly.
>
> **From what has been discussed above, we may safely draw the conclusion that we should embrace positive attitudes.** **As the modern college students, we are supposed to promote this good quality.** **And I am quite sure that if we can keep optimistic, there would be a promising future awaiting us.**

**槽位标注**：第 1 段＝总体描述（As is vividly depicted + 双主体 show different attitudes）＋细节 1（Noticeably）＋细节 2（In contrast + 分词 reaching for）＋文字说明（Just as the title goes）；第 2 段＝主题句（Undoubtedly ... that 同位语从句）＋总领句（no one can deny）＋三条展开（First and foremost / In addition / In the end）；第 3 段＝套话（From what has been discussed above）＋态度（be supposed to）＋展望（if we can ... , there would be ...）。

#### 二、示范二：负面话题·社会现象（"养老足球赛"题，195 词）

> As is vividly depicted in the picture, four adult children are standing in front of their own goals, kicking their aged father like a ball.
>
> **Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a social problem much concerned in our current life that some adult children try to shun the responsibility of supporting their aged parents.** **The reasons for the phenomenon may be complicated, but we can, to a large extent, ascribe it to the following interrelated factors.** **To begin with**, this undesirable phenomenon of maltreatment of the elders can be attributed to the absence of people's consciousness of traditional value of family in modern life. **Moreover**, relevant laws and regulations might not have been strictly enforced to prevent people from shrugging off their social obligations.
>
> **It is high time we placed great emphasis on the unpleasant phenomenon.** **To begin with**, relevant laws and regulations should be made to severely punish those who shun the responsibility. **In addition**, it is essential to cultivate the awareness among the young adults that respecting and supporting the old is always a virtue of Chinese culture. **Only in this way can we curb the current evil phenomenon revealed in the picture.**

**槽位标注**：首段第 1 句把**总体描述与细节合并**（总述 + kicking their aged father like a ball），题字不明显时可省第 4 句——**首段省句是控字数最快的杠杆**；第 2 段＝主题句（Undoubtedly ... that）＋原因总领句（ascribe it to the following interrelated factors）＋原因 1（To begin with · **意识缺失**）＋原因 2（Moreover · **法律法规**）；第 3 段＝套话（It is high time）＋措施两条（法律惩罚 + 意识培养）＋包装结尾（**Only 部分倒装**回扣图画）。

#### 三、宏观社会版：只换两处（环境 / 文化 / 经济类话题）

1. **主体段总领句换成**：**It is widely shared that protecting the environment can promote both individual growth and the whole social progress.**
2. **结论段第二层换成**：**As the modern college students, we are bound to benefit from a harmonious environment and are accountable for promoting green and sustainable development.**
3. 其余骨架不动（主题句、展开层次词、第三层展望均通用）——**这就是"一套模板打通两类话题"的用法**。

### （八）考场自查清单（写完 60 秒过一遍）

1. **审题三问**对了吗（正面 / 负面、个人 / 社会、单主体 / 双主体）
2. **首段 2-4 句**，层次是"总体描述 → 细节描述 → 文字说明"
3. **主体段**有主题句 + 总领句 + 2-3 条展开，且有层次词（First and foremost / In addition / To begin with / Moreover）
4. **结论段三层齐全**：套话总结 → 态度决心或采取措施 → 包装结尾
5. **字数 160-200 词**（段落配比 40~50 / 70~90 / 45~60）
6. **保底语法**：谓语完整、主谓一致、时态一致（描述图画用现在时）

**高频错点**：

| <nobr>易错点</nobr> | 正确写法 |
|:---|:---|
| 被动漏 be | importance **should be attached** to ...（不是 should attach to） |
| 主语数不一致 | **Recent years have** witnessed ...；**A survey has** shown ... |
| if 从句用将来时 | **主将从现**：If we **get** fully prepared, we **can** ... |
| Only 忘记倒装 | **Only in this way can we** curb ...（只倒主句，从句不倒） |
| 同位语从句写成定语从句 | that 后必须是**完整句子**（that 不作成分） |
| It is high time 后接原形 | It is high time that we **placed** ...（虚拟用过去式） |
| 比较级表最高级写反 | it **couldn't be more** helpful **than** to do sth.（"没有比……更有益的了"） |
| although 与 but 连用 | 二者只留一个 |
| 分词状语主语不一致 | 从句 / 分词的主语须与主句主语一致，否则用 **with 复合结构**或独立主格 |
| elder / eldest 混用 | 两者比较用 elder，三者以上之最用 **eldest**（the **eldest** son） |
