<h2 align="center">应用文·句式汇总（书信类）</h2>

> **本文定位**：汇总**应用文（书信 / 通知）**的句式——与论述型作文的 `写作句式.md` **分体裁存放**（论述型按写作功能编排，本文按**书信部位**编排：称呼 → 首段 → 中间段 → 末段 → 署名）。目前收录**申请信**一讲，其余信函类型留占位，后续讲次补齐。
> **用法**：写应用文时按"部位"取句——开头从（四）一取，主体从（四）二取，结尾从（四）三取；称呼与署名从（三）取；写完对（五）的中文信号查一遍。

### （一）应用文总纲（先对表）

| <nobr>项目</nobr> | <nobr>要求</nobr> |
|:---|:---|
| <nobr>题型</nobr> | 英语二小作文，应用性短文（信函 / 通知 / 备忘录 / 报告） |
| <nobr>分值 · 字数</nobr> | **10 分**，**100 词左右**（80-120 都安全），10 分钟量级 |
| <nobr>大纲四条</nobr> | ① 语法拼写标点正确、用词恰当 ② 遵循特定文体格式 ③ 结构统一连贯 ④ **语域**恰当 |
| <nobr>语域</nobr> | 按交际对象选正式 / 一般 / 非正式；**申请、投诉、建议类一律正式**：不用缩写、不用口语、不用感叹号 |
| <nobr>三段</nobr> | 首段 = 身份 + 目的；中间段 = 细化题目要求；末段 = 感谢 + 期待回复 |

### （二）书信格式：称呼 / 正文 / 署名

#### 一、称呼（顶格，后接逗号）

**熟人**：

1. **Dear + 名字，**（Dear Li Ming,）
2. **Dear Mr. / Ms. + 姓氏，**
3. **Dear Prof. / Dr. / President + 姓氏，**（有头衔用头衔，最正式）

**陌生人**：

1. **Dear Sir / Madam，**
2. **To whom it may concern，**（敬启者，最正式）

#### 二、正文：三段

1. **首段**：问候 / 表明身份 / 收到回复 + **表明写信目的**。
2. **中间段**：**细化题目要求**（申请信=理由+资格；投诉信=经过；咨询信=问题）。
3. **末段**：**期待回复 / 祝福 / 客套话**。

#### 三、署名（右下）

1. **Yours sincerely,**（知道收信人姓名时）
2. **Yours faithfully,**（不知道收信人、用 Dear Sir/Madam 时）
3. 换行写名字：考试一律 **Li Ming**（不得写真实姓名）。

### （三）通用句（各类信函共用）

1. **I am writing to ...**（表明写信目的 · 最常用的开头骨架）
    - I am writing **to apply for** a membership in your musical band.
2. **I am writing in reply to ... / with regard to ...**（回信或就某事写信 · 正式）
    - I am writing **in reply to** your advertisement on the campus website.
3. **I would like to ... / I wonder if you could ...**（提出请求 · 客气版）
    - **I wonder if you could** send me more information about the program.
4. **Thank you for your time and consideration.**（感谢阅读 · 通用结尾）
    - **Thank you for your time and consideration.**
5. **I am looking forward to your early reply.**（期待回复 · 最常用）
    - I am **looking forward to** your early reply.（to 是介词，后接名词 / -ing）
6. **If you need any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me.**（提供后续联系 · 通用）
    - If you need any further information, please **do not hesitate to** contact me.

### （四）申请信专用句式

#### 一、首段：表明申请目的

1. **I am writing to apply for the post of ... advertised in ...**（求职 · 基础版）
    - I am writing to apply for the post of **sales manager advertised in China Daily**.
2. **I am writing to express my interest in your recently advertised position for ...**（求职 · 亮点版）
    - I am writing to express my interest in your recently advertised position for **a research assistant**.
3. **As a + 身份 + who ..., I am writing to apply for a membership in ...**（加入组织 · 范文版，身份与目的一句说完）
    - **As a music lover who has been playing various instruments ever since I was in elementary school, I am writing to apply for a membership in your musical band.**
4. **I am writing to apply for admission to your ... program.**（求学 · 申请学位）
    - I am writing to apply for **admission to your master's program in economics**.

#### 二、中间段：自述理由 + 介绍资格

1. **Upon graduation, I first worked as ... The following job was ... and currently I am working for ... I feel I have the necessary qualification and experience needed for the position of ...**（工作经历型）
    - **Upon graduation, I first worked as a clerk. The following job was a sales assistant, and currently I am working for a trading company. I feel I have the necessary qualification and experience needed for the position of sales manager.**
2. **I am now studying at ... and will soon be graduated as a/an ... major. Under the influence of the courses I have taken, I have developed a special interest in ... and I understand your university has a good program in those fields.**（在读申请型）
    - **I am now studying at Peking University and will soon be graduated as a computer science major. Under the influence of the courses I have taken, I have developed a special interest in artificial intelligence, and I understand your university has a good program in those fields.**
3. **My ... strongly recommended that I ... , telling me that it would benefit me in many years to come.**（第三方推荐 · 范文句，比自己夸自己自然）
    - **My music teacher, Prof. Zhang, strongly recommended that I attend such a renowned organization, telling me that it would benefit me in many years to come.**
4. **I learned ... at ... and ... at ...**（用具体经历支撑资格 · 短句排比）
    - **I learned piano at eight and flute at twelve.**
5. **I hope I can ... in your team—with so many ... around me, I will surely become more ... and ....**（加入后的愿景 · 收束中间段）
    - **I hope I can grow up into a good saxophone player in your team—with so many music lovers around me, I will surely become more creative and enthusiastic.**

#### 三、末段：感谢 + 期待回复

1. **Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to meeting you in the near future.**（通用 · 基础版）
    - **Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to meeting you in the near future.**
2. **If my application could be taken into favorable consideration, I would be most grateful.**（虚拟语气 · 客气版，范文句）
    - **If my application could be taken into favorable consideration, I would be most grateful.**
3. **I would greatly appreciate receiving copies of the required application forms and any instructions you may be so kind to give me regarding my possible admission.**（求学 · 索取材料）
    - **I would greatly appreciate receiving copies of the required application forms.**
4. **I am looking forward to your early reply.**（期待回复 · 可与上句连用）

### （五）中文信号 → 句式速查

1. **"我写信是为了申请……"** → I am writing to apply for ...（（四）一）
    - I am writing to apply for the post of ...
2. **"我对贵方近期招聘的……职位很感兴趣"** → I am writing to express my interest in your recently advertised position for ...（（四）一）
3. **"作为……爱好者，我写信申请加入……"** → As a ... lover who ..., I am writing to apply for a membership in ...（（四）一）
4. **"毕业后我先做……，目前在……工作"** → Upon graduation, I first worked as ... Currently I am working for ...（（四）二）
5. **"我目前就读于……，即将毕业于……专业"** → I am now studying at ... and will soon be graduated as a ... major.（（四）二）
6. **"我的……强烈推荐我……"** → My ... strongly recommended that I ..., telling me that it would benefit me ...（（四）二）
7. **"我认为自己具备该职位所需的资格与经验"** → I feel I have the necessary qualification and experience needed for ...（（四）二）
8. **"感谢考虑我的申请"** → Thank you for considering my application.（（四）三）
9. **"如蒙青睐，我将不胜感激"** → If my application could be taken into favorable consideration, I would be most grateful.（（四）三）
10. **"期待早日回复"** → I am looking forward to your early reply.（（四）三）
11. **"如需更多信息请随时联系我"** → If you need any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me.（（三））

### （七）使用四原则

1. **格式先行**：称呼逗号、正文三段、Yours sincerely + Li Ming——格式分先拿稳，再谈句式。
2. **一句一功能**：首段只点目的，中间段只给理由资格，末段只感谢期待；不要把三段写成一段。
3. **正式语域**：不用缩写、不用口语、不用感叹号；`I would like to` 优于 `I want to`。
4. **字数务实**：100 词左右——首段 1-2 句、中间段 2-4 句、末段 1-2 句，恰好 8-12 句。
