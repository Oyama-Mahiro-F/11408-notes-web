<h2 align="center">论述型作文·主体段与结论段</h2>

> **本讲核心**：Directions 的第 2)、3) 项要求落在**第二段（主体段）与第三段（结论段）**。主体段 = **第一层 段落主题句 + 第二层 主题展开论述**（≈6 句）；结论段 = **第一层 套话总结归纳 + 第二层 态度决心或采取措施 + 第三层 包装结尾、展望未来**。两类话题各有主线——**正面话题走"意义分析"，负面话题走"原因分析"**，再用**举例 / 数据 / 对比**三类辅助论述撑起细节。
> **已成稿模板**：本讲与 `论述型作文-图画作文首段.md` 已压成 `论述型作文-三段写作模板.md`（三段十二句骨架 + 三篇范文 + 替换词库 + 自查清单），考场可直接套用；另有速成讲 `论述型作文-图画作文速成.md`（中间段**三类模板**：原因分析 / 意义分析 / 利弊分析，尾段**两类模板**：建议措施 / 总结概括）。

### （一）本讲定位：三项要求 ↔ 三个段落

Directions 原文（160-200 词）：

> 1) **describe the picture briefly**, 2) **explain its intended meaning** and 3) **give your comments**.

课上在试题上标注了两处衔接：第 2) 项 → **para.1 尾 / para.2 首**；第 3) 项 → **para.2 & 3**。

| <nobr>要求</nobr> | <nobr>落位</nobr> | 本讲覆盖 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 1) describe the picture briefly | 第一段（首段） | 见 `论述型作文-图画作文首段.md` |
| 2) explain its intended meaning | **para.1 尾 / para.2 首** | **本讲·1.1 段落主题句** |
| 3) give your comments | **para.2 & 3** | **本讲·1.2 主题论证 + 二、结论段** |

**课程纲要**：

```
第二段（主体段）：写作框架 / 句式 / 素材
第三段（结论段）：写作框架 / 句式
```

> **本讲性质**：以**段落框架 + 句式模板**为主（不是主题素材）——故本文与《写作句式.md》按功能节同步收录。

### （二）主体段框架

#### 一、框架：两层、六句

```
主体段框架（≈6 句）
├─ 第一层：段落主题句     ← 点明主题（现象 or 抽象观点/主题）
└─ 第二层：主题展开论述   ← 意义分析 or 原因分析（+ 三类辅助论证）
```

#### 二、两类话题，两条主线

| <nobr>话题类型</nobr> | <nobr>第二层主线</nobr> | <nobr>段落结构</nobr> |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **正面话题** | **意义分析** | 意义分析总领句 + 具体意义分析（2-3 条） |
| **负面话题** | **原因分析** | 原因分析总领句 + 具体原因分析（2-3 条） |

**三类辅助论述方式**（把 2-3 条撑成段）：**举例、数据、对比**。

> **判据**：题干/图画指向"好品质、好现象"→ 正面话题走意义分析；指向"不良风气、社会问题"→ 负面话题走原因分析。**两套主线不要混用**（负面话题写"意义"会跑题）。

#### 三、【考题体验】乐观（半瓶水漫画）

- **主题词**：optimism / being optimistic / having a positive mind / a right attitude
- **配图**：一人扶额低头"全完了！"，另一人笑着捡瓶"幸好还剩点儿！"

**段落一览**（四句，第一层 + 第二层）：

1. **The symbolic meaning of the picture is to show us importance should be attached to an optimistic mindset.**（第一层·主题句）
2. **First and foremost, optimism is one of the most essential qualities that anyone who desires to achieve success should possess.**（第二层·定性与意义）
3. **In addition, by being optimistic, we can face challenges with confidence, keep sober and figure out effective measures even in desperate situations.**（第二层·做法与结果）
4. **In the end, a positive mind will enable us to achieve our objectives more smoothly and realize our dreams more rapidly.**（第二层·收束）

> **技巧**：第二层的 2-3 条用 **First and foremost → In addition → In the end** 串起来，层次立刻清楚，不必每句另起句式。

#### 四、主体段创作小结（三条）

1. **如何提出主题 / 中心句**——见（三）段落主题句三式
2. **两类常用框架**：意义分析 or 原因分析——**重点掌握 ① 论述句型 ② 填充角度**
3. **三类辅助论证方式**：举例、数据、对比

### （三）1.1 段落主题句（第一层）

#### 一、正确审题的关键

1. **借助标题 / 中文提示 + 图画元素分析**：判断本题是**社会现象**类还是**人生哲理**类
2. **主题定位**：把握**象征含义、以小见大**——落到 **个体 / 群体思想意识形态**
    - 常见落点：`a positive mind / attitude`、`appropriate manners`、`proper understanding of ...`、`clear awareness of ...`

> **说明**：漫画里的"半瓶水""温室花朵""一张网"，本身不是主题，**画中人/物的态度与意识**才是主题——这一步定了，主题句才有落点。

#### 二、主题句常用句式三式

1. **The symbolic / intended meaning of the picture is to show us that importance should be attached to ...**（目的/意义型 · "这幅图的意义是，我们应对……予以重视"）
    - **The symbolic meaning of the picture is to show us that importance should be attached to** an optimistic mindset.
2. **The picture has (subtly) reflected / revealed the social phenomenon prevalent / common among the public nowadays that + 句子**（同位语从句 · 揭示社会现象）
    - The picture has **subtly** revealed the social phenomenon **prevalent** among the public nowadays **that** more and more people ...
3. **Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a problem / topic much concerned in our current life that + 句子**（同位语从句 · **最常用**，"毫无疑问，这幅画本质上揭示了一个当下备受关注的问题/话题"）
    - **Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a social problem much concerned in our current life that** some adult children try to **shun** the responsibility of supporting their aged parents.

> **考点**：`that + 句子` 作**同位语从句**（解释前面的 problem / topic / phenomenon），从句**意思完整**；若 that 后缺成分则是定语从句——两者分水岭见《写作句式.md》功能二。
> **易错点**：`importance should be attached to` 中 **should be attached** 是被动（"重视"是被施加到某物上的），不要写成 importance should attach to。

#### 三、主题句示例：网络对人际关系的影响

1. **主题词**：网络对于人际关系的影响——**好与坏**（一分为二）
2. **中文主题句**：网络貌似可以让人轻松建立联系，但也使人们失去了现实的交往
3. **英文主题句（同位语从句版）**：
    - **Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a problem much concerned in our current life that** it seems that the Internet enables people to build connections easily, **but meanwhile it also leads to the loss of real social interactions**.
    - 替换：`... but meanwhile **it also alienates them**.`（疏远）
4. **英文主题句（意义型版）**：
    - **Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a topic much concerned in our current life—the impact of the Internet on social relationship.**
    - **The intended meaning of the picture is to bring us to a full awareness of the impact of the Internet on social relationship.**

**配图**："网络的'近'与'远'"（蜘蛛网中每个格子都是人/家庭场景）——**网越密，"近"是联系方便，"远"是现实交往淡漠**，正好一句写两面。

#### 四、主题句里的"应该 / 重视"词块

| <nobr>语义</nobr> | 可用表达 |
|:---|:---|
| <nobr>应该</nobr> | should；ought to；have to / need to；**be expected to do**；**be supposed to do** |
| <nobr>重视</nobr> | **attach great importance to sth.**；**pay due attention to sth.**；**place much emphasis on sth.**；stress sth. |

> **技巧**：同一句主题句里"应该"只出现一次——`importance should be attached to`、`we are supposed to`、`we are expected to` 轮换用，避免通篇 should。

### （四）1.2 主题论证（第二层）

**两大主线 + 三类辅助**：

```
主体论述方法
├─ 意义分析（正面话题）
└─ 原因分析（负面话题）
辅助论述方法拓展
├─ 举例说明
├─ 数据统计
└─ 正反论证（对比）
```

#### 一、正面话题：意义分析总领句

1. **Definitely, no one can deny the significance of ...**（"当然，没有人能够否认……的重要性"）
2. **It is widely shared that ... can promote both individual growth and the whole social progress.**（"普遍认为……能同时促进个人成长与社会进步"）

> **技巧**：总领句只交代"这件事有意义"，**具体意义留给后面 2-3 条**——总领句写太满，后面就没得展开。

#### 二、具体意义分析的两个填充角度

**① 宏观维度**（一件事带来的好处，多从这几个词切入）：

| <nobr>维度</nobr> | 英文 |
|:---|:---|
| <nobr>和谐</nobr> | harmony（形容词 harmonious） |
| <nobr>可持续</nobr> | sustainability |
| <nobr>挑战</nobr> | challenge |
| <nobr>困难</nobr> | difficulty / hardship |
| <nobr>压力</nobr> | stress / pressure |
| <nobr>效率</nobr> | efficiency |
| <nobr>效果</nobr> | effectiveness |

**② 个人发展**（目标 / 梦想 / 成功 / 自我实现）——三条子角度：

1. **获得 / 迎接 / 抓住机遇**
2. **面对 / 应对困难、挑战、压力**
3. **又快又好**——效率 / 效果

**配套词块**：

| <nobr>语义</nobr> | 词块 |
|:---|:---|
| <nobr>发展</nobr> | development / advancement / growth / progress |
| <nobr>实现</nobr> | realize / actualize / make ... come true / bring ... into reality |
| <nobr>取得</nobr> | achieve / accomplish / fulfill / obtain / attain |
| <nobr>目标 / 梦想</nobr> | goal / dream |
| <nobr>成功 / 机遇</nobr> | success / opportunity |

#### 三、意义分析句子表达示例

1. **Optimism is one of the most essential qualities that anyone who desires to achieve success should possess.**（**主题词定性**——把主题词定性为"最重要的品质之一"）
2. **A positive mind will enable us to achieve our objectives more smoothly and realize our dreams more rapidly.**（**表达公式 = 主题 + 句式 + 角度 + 修饰**：enable sb to do + 角度（目标/梦想）+ 副词修饰 more smoothly / more rapidly）
3. **By being optimistic, we can face challenges with confidence, keep sober and figure out effective measures even in desperate situations.**（**by doing sth, sb can do sth**——方式 + 结果）

> **技巧**：给"意义"句加修饰最省力——`achieve our objectives **more smoothly**`、`realize our dreams **more rapidly**`、`even in desperate situations`，一句就写满。
> **易错点**：`qualities that anyone who desires to achieve success should possess` 里**双从句套叠**（that 定从 + anyone who 定从），写的时候先搭好 `anyone who ... should possess`，再把 qualities 挂上去。

#### 四、负面话题：原因分析总领句

1. **Several factors, both individual and social, may be responsible for the negative phenomenon.**（"个人的和社会的因素都造成这种负面现象"）
2. **The reasons for the phenomenon may be complicated, but we can, to a large extent, ascribe it to the following interrelated factors.**（"原因可能复杂，但很大程度上可归结为以下相互关联的因素"）

> **技巧**：②里 `can, to a large extent,` 是**插入成分**（用逗号隔开），写出来句子立刻有"客观、克制"的味道——这正是考研作文要的腔调。

#### 五、具体原因分析的三类常见维度

**先选大方向**：

| <nobr>层面</nobr> | 常见落点 |
|:---|:---|
| <nobr>个人</nobr> | 自私自利、道德缺失、意识不足＝忽视……、缺少自我约束/管理 |
| <nobr>社会</nobr> | 制度不完善、监管不力、媒体引导宣传不足 |

**再套句式**（三个示例句，对应上面三类原因）：

1. **意识缺失**——**The phenomenon (that ...) can be attributed to the absence / lack / insufficiency of people's consciousness / awareness (of sth.)**
    - The phenomenon can be **attributed to the absence of people's consciousness** of traditional value of family in modern life.
2. **利益驱使**——**To gain excessive private profits, sb tend to do sth without much consideration of the rights / interests of sb.**
    - **To gain excessive private profits**, some enterprises **tend to** discharge waste water **without much consideration of the interests of** local residents.
3. **法律法规**——**Relevant laws and regulations might not have been strictly enforced to restrict / prevent people from doing sth.**
    - **Relevant laws and regulations might not have been strictly enforced to prevent** people **from** shrugging off their social obligations.

> **记忆**：负面话题三段归因——**意识（主观）→ 利益（动机）→ 法律（外在约束）**，任选 2-3 条即可成段。

#### 六、【考题体验】养老话题（"足球赛"漫画）展开论述

- **配图**：四位子女各守一个球门、把老父亲当球踢开（"大儿子/二儿子/三儿子/小女儿/老父亲"）——**养老"足球赛"**。
- **主题句**：
    - **Undoubtedly, the drawing has essentially revealed a social problem much concerned in our current life that** some adult children try to **escape from / get rid of / shun** the responsibility / obligation of supporting their aged parents.
- **展开论述**：
    1. **The reasons for the phenomenon may be complicated, but we can, to a large extent, ascribe it to the following interrelated factors.**（总领句）
    2. **To begin with, it is apparent that this undesirable phenomenon of maltreatment of the elders can be attributed to the absence of people's consciousness of traditional value of family in modern life.**（意识缺失）
    3. **Moreover, relevant laws and regulations might not have been strictly enforced to prevent people from shrugging off their social obligations like supporting the old.**（法律约束）

> **技巧**：总领句给"以下因素"，展开句用 **To begin with → Moreover** 逐条落实，最后一条落在**法律/监管**上，段落就有了"从个人到社会"的层次。

### （五）辅助论述方法（三类：举例 / 数据 / 对比）

#### 一、举例说明

**引出例子的句式**：

1. **Numerous examples can be given, but this / these will suffice.**（"例子举不胜举，但下面这个/这些就足够了"）
2. **I can think of no better illustration than the following one(s).**（"我想不出比下面这个/这些更好的例子了"）

**例题：爱心（Love is the most valuable when one is in difficulty.）**

- **主题句**：**Love is the most valuable when one is in difficulty.**
- **举例论证**：
    1. **For instance, when someone is starving to death, just a little food and water from you may save his life.**
    2. **Or when a little girl in a poor rural area drops out of school because of poverty, just a small sum of money from a kind man may support her to finish her schooling and even change her life.**
    3. **Love is just like a lamp in a dark place where light is most needed.**（回扣主题句）

> **技巧**：例子写两个就够，第三个位置用来**回扣主题**（"爱心就像暗处的灯"）——段落收得自然，也不必另写总结句。

#### 二、数据统计法

**句式**：

1. **According to the recent survey / data / figure / statistic / study by the Chinese Academy of Social Science, ...% of sb do sth.**
2. **A survey conducted online by a non-governmental organization has shown that roughly / more than / over ...% of sb do sth.**

**例题：追星现象**

- **主题句**：**Recent years have witnessed the prevalence of star-worshipping among the Chinese youth.**
- **拓展句**：**According to the recent survey by the Chinese Academy of Social Science, 75.1% of teenagers in China have at least an idol from film or music stars.**

> **注意**：数字只需"看起来可信"——给具体到小数点的百分比（**75.1%**）比"many"更有说服力；`roughly / more than / over` 用来表示约数。
> **易错点**：`Recent years` 作主语是**复数**，须用 **have witnessed**（讲义原文作 has witnessed，属笔误）；`A survey` 为单数，须用 **has shown**。另 `recent years` 前后不再加 in。

#### 三、对比论证法（正反论证）

**常用过渡短语**：**On the contrary**、**In contrast** 等。

- **主题句**：**An optimistic attitude does much good to us.**
- **拓展句**：
    - **Optimistic people can always see the upsides of a matter, which brings them a good mood and fruitful rewards.**
    - **On the contrary, pessimistic ones tend to keep complaining about misfortunes and difficulties, thus losing confidence and missing chances.**

> **技巧**：对比段的标准配比 = **一正 + 一反**（正面人如何 → On the contrary，反面人如何），反面句用 `thus doing` 收尾点出后果，一段就写满。

### （六）结论段写作方法（第三段）

#### 一、写作框架：三层

```
结论段写作框架
├─ 第一层：套话，总结归纳 / 重申主题
├─ 第二层：态度决心或采取措施
└─ 第三层：包装结尾，展望未来
```

#### 二、第一层：套话，总结归纳

1. **From what has been discussed above, we may safely draw the conclusion that ...**（"综上所述，我们可以稳妥地得出结论……"）
2. **It is high time that we placed great emphasis on ...**（"是时候高度重视……了"；**虚拟语气**，that 后谓语用**过去式** placed）

#### 三、第二层：态度决心或采取措施

**（1）意义价值类**

| <nobr>落点</nobr> | 句式 |
|:---|:---|
| <nobr>微观个人</nobr> | ① **To conclude, for a healthy personal growth, it couldn't be more helpful than to do sth.**（"对个人健康成长而言，没有比做某事更有益的了"——比较级表最高级）<br>② **And I am determined to be more + adj. so that there would be a promising future awaiting me.** |
| <nobr>宏观社会</nobr> | ① **As the modern college students, we are bound to benefit from ... and are accountable for promoting ... .**（"我们必将从中受益，也有责任推动……"）<br>② **I am quite sure that if everybody were to ..., there would be a promising future awaiting us.**（**虚拟语气** were to / would be） |

> **说明**：`——微观个人` 用于个人成长类话题；`——宏观社会` 用于**环境资源、文化、经济**等社会层面话题。选错落点会显得格局不合。

**（2）问题解决类**

- **On the one hand, it is essential to enhance / cultivate the awareness among the public that sth is vital for everyone.**
- **On the other hand, we should appeal to the authorities to make strict laws to curb the negative impact of this problem and severely punish those who disobey or break the rules.**

> **技巧**：问题解决类就是"**提高意识 + 诉诸立法**"两条腿——`enhance / cultivate the awareness among the public`、`appeal to the authorities`、`curb the negative impact`、`severely punish those who ...` 四个词块自由搭配。

#### 四、第三层：包装结尾，展望未来

1. **I believe in joint efforts we humans can overcome this difficulty and we will definitely enjoy a more promising future.**（共同愿景式）
2. **Only in this way can we curb the current evil phenomenon revealed in the picture.**（部分倒装式 · 回扣图画）

> **注意**：② 是 **Only + 状语置于句首 → 主句部分倒装**（can we curb）；判断句首是不是 only 修饰状语，是就必须倒装。

#### 五、结尾段两个示例

**【结尾段写作示例 1】——意义价值类**（乐观话题）

> ① **From what has been discussed above, we may safely draw the conclusion that we should embrace positive attitudes.** ② **As the modern college students, we are supposed to promote this good quality.** ③ **And I am quite sure that if we can keep optimistic, there would be a promising future awaiting us.**

**拆解**：第 ① 句 = 第一层套话总结；第 ② 句 = 第二层态度决心（`be supposed to`）；第 ③ 句 = 第三层展望未来（`if we can ... , there would be ...`）。

**【结尾段写作示例 2】——问题解决型**（养老话题）

> ① **It is high time we placed great emphasis on the unpleasant phenomenon.** ② **To begin with, relevant laws and regulations should be made to severely punish those who shun the responsibility.** ③ **In addition, it is essential to cultivate the awareness among the young adults that respecting and supporting the old will always be a virtue of Chinese culture that everyone should carry forward.**

**拆解**：第 ① 句 = 第一层重申主题（`It is high time we placed ...`）；第 ②③ 句 = 第二层采取措施（**法律惩罚 + 意识培养**，用 To begin with / In addition 并列）。

### （七）本讲总结

**知识结构**：

```
主体段与结论段
├─ 一、本讲定位（Directions ②③ → para.1尾/para.2首、para.2&3）
├─ 二、主体段框架（≈6 句）

└─ 六、结论段写作方法
```
